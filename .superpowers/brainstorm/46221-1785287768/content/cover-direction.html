How should the eight-book cover set be finished?

The current six covers share dark archival imagery, gold serif type, period documents, and a recurring magnifying glass—but Books 3 and 4 visibly depart from the dominant composition.

A — Extend the current set

Create Books 7 and 8 in the dominant established style; keep Books 1–6 unchanged. Fastest and preserves already-published branding.

B — Harmonize where practical

Keep published Books 1–2, preserve approved release covers when sound, create Books 7–8, and normalize metadata, sizing, and obvious outliers without a wholesale rebrand.

C — Redesign all eight

Build one tightly unified visual system across the entire series. Strongest shelf cohesion, but replaces published branding and expands approval and validation work.

Recommendation: B. It protects the two published books while giving the finished set stronger technical and visual consistency.
