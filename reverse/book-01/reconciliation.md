# Book 1 Reconciliation — reverse-engineered canon vs. production bibles

Companion to `canon-decisions.md` and `reverse-scaffold-analysis.md`.
Compared: `reverse/book-01/canon/*` (retrofit from published text) against
`books/book-01/bible/*` (Book 1 planning bible) and `series-bible/*` (series
living canon). Precedence: **published text > production bible > inference** —
Book 1 is frozen, so where they disagree, the series plans around the text.

**Verdict: the retrofit and the bibles agree on every load-bearing fact** —
who killed Pike and why, the fraud's mechanics, the character arcs, the
motif system, the single-POV constraint. The divergences below are
secondary but real; each needs to be honored (not silently "fixed") when
Books 3–8 canon is next touched.

## Divergences (text wins; bibles superseded on these points)

1. **Mae Hartwell's age.** The published text pins her at ~59–60 at Book 1
   ("nine years old" + "fifty years," ch4 — exact arithmetic). The Book 1
   bible and the series recurring-characters file both say "early
   seventies." Superseded. **Corollary:** the bible's "she changed Cross's
   diapers" is void (impossible against Cross's mid-fifties); use the
   bible's compatible alternative, "she's known him since he was a deputy."
   Future books: Mae is ~60 at Book 1, ~62 by Book 5.
2. **The keystone is not a Bible.** The Book 1 bible's world.md says Wren
   hid his record "in the town's foundational land records **and in a
   family Bible he annotated** before fleeing." The finished book instead
   makes the keystone an **untitled clothbound volume of regional survey
   records** (Society collections room, 1962 Ralston lot), and the family
   Bible in play is **Mae's grandmother's own record** (parlor shelf, ch7).
   Future books must not resurrect "Wren's Bible."
3. **Callie's birth year.** Series timeline says "~1985 born / ~1994 glass";
   the text's arithmetic (tea since 19 + sixteen years) gives **~1990**,
   and every character file says "early-to-mid thirties." The timeline's
   birth-year line is the outlier — superseded. (The timeline's other
   anchors — shop founded ~1985, Eleanor dies ~2024, Book 1 present late
   2025 — are consistent with the text and stand.)
4. **Fenn timing.** The Book 1 bible planned "Gus learns of the manuscript
   ~2 weeks before"; the finished register gives **8 days** (with Pike
   showing him findings ~6 days out). Text wins; the register dates in
   canon/timeline.md are authoritative.
5. **Fraud scope.** The bibles say "hundreds of acres from several
   families"; the text is precise: **~140 acres, five families**. Text
   wins (the bibles' vagueness was planning-stage).

## Confirmations (bible facts the text supports — now canon with dual sourcing)

- Mae's promise to Eleanor ("look after Callie") — in both; the text's ch6
  narration ("the woman she had promised to look after") confirms it.
- Ralston's hands canon — the bible's revised note (over-control is the
  tell; crack only when being read; never a private-alone tremble) matches
  the published ch3/ch5 exactly.
- The revelation cadence (bible story-memory's canon note: ch2 withholds,
  ch3 fraud, ch4 murder, ch5 corroboration + evidentiary chain) matches
  the published text — including the ch5 reframe the bible demanded ("she
  already knew that; she had read the accusation in the 1843 ledger two
  days ago"). The manuscript analyzed is the final revised text.
- The floorboard payoff structure (habit → ch8 deliberate step-on) matches.
- Cross, Eli, and Pike characterizations; the sheriff's department shape;
  the council/sheriff relationship.
- Book 2 carry-forward facts: Gus imprisoned, Society closed, town wounded
  and knowing the truth — consistent with Book 1's ending state.

## Gaps only the bibles could fill (now canon — see canon-decisions.md)

Ages (Gus early sixties, Cross mid-fifties, Eli early twenties), Callie's
appearance, Eleanor's surname/profession, Eli's backstory (bus, dropout,
father), Mae's blood-line Hartwell identity, Cross's father's creed, the
department's size, Ralston Hall's tour custom, the town's founding decade
and population, story-present = late 2025, and Gus's ultimate imprisonment.
