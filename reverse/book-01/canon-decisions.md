- **Eleanor's death revisitable** — canon says natural; the door stays unlocked (see #6).
- **Jacob Wren's fate** after fleeing (1843) — never stated; open by design.
- **J. Fenn's identity** — first initial only; no book through Book 5 consumes the thread; available.
- **The poison** — never named; an unnamed "compound" per the state lab. Later books may name it; do not invent retroactively.
- **Foxed Page landlord** — ownership unnamed; Ralston's lease leverage reads as positional (council/bank goodwill), not documented ownership.
- **The botanical annotator + the visitor at the door** (ch8) — Book 2's engine; owned by Book 2.
- **Land-restitution legal aftermath** ("could be challenged in court", ch6) — series-level consequence, unspent on the page.

3. **Callie's birth year**: series timeline "~1985" vs. text-derived ~1990 — text wins (#1).
