## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The tea tastes wrong
- **Setting**: The Foxed Page, morning after; rain, still rain.
- **Characters**: Callie; later Dalton Cross.
- **Purpose**: Grief made sensory; then Cross's deposition-cadence interview — Pike's routine, the Eli shouting match, "Anxiety isn't guilt" — and Callie's instinctive withholding of the annotations.
- **Value shift**: `cooperative → guarded`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. Two cups, both tasting of "cold concrete and a paper cup tipped on its side."
  2. Cross at the doorframe: the witness list, the misfiled document argument, "Most people who act guilty have a reason."
  3. The held-back knowledge ("the way she held back the breath before a sneeze"); the hairline crack between them — his methods can't weigh a margin note.
- **Exit state**: Callie is alone with what she hasn't told him.

### Scene 2: Mae's visit
- **Setting**: The shop; lavender and rain; soup.
- **Characters**: Callie, Mae Hartwell.
- **Purpose**: Comfort as interrogation. Mae's hands straighten shelves; her questions escalate from "what was he researching" to the too-specific "where are his papers now"; the one-second stillness at the word *threads*.
- **Value shift**: `nurtured → watched`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. Soup, no permission asked; "Sit. Eat."
  2. "Some old things are better left where the roots have settled" — the stillness Callie reads as a woman holding something shut.
  3. The question that isn't the first question; "filed... without deciding what it meant."
- **Exit state**: Mae leaves; the lavender stays; Callie looks at the door a long time.

### Scene 3: The archives, pp. 31–36
- **Setting**: The town archives basement, 3:47 pm, signed log; Pike's table by the east vault.
- **Characters**: Callie (Pike present in traces).
- **Purpose**: The discovery that converts grief to purpose — his untouched table, the afraid hand, the note ("Confirmed — S.R. signature matches forged survey lines... J.W. did not lie... See expanded findings, MS pp. 31-36"), and the clean tear where six pages were.
- **Value shift**: `grief → purpose`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The untouched table; "he had been alive, and he had been afraid, and he had not told her how much."
  2. Reading the note; *S.R.* — the R known, the S unplaced.
  3. The binding's story: "Six pages. The six pages that mattered."
- **Exit state**: "She opened the first of Jacob Wren's annotated surveys and began, at last, to hunt."
