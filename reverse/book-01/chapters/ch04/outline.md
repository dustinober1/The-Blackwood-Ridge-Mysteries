## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The 1843 ledger
- **Setting**: The archives, Wednesday morning; the back third of the ledger, a margin no wider than her thumb.
- **Characters**: Callie; Wren's breaking hand.
- **Purpose**: The historical murder decoded — "Minister moriturus. Equus, via nota. Non casus." → the name written full and half-scored: Silas Ralston ordered Rev. Thomas Hartwell killed; the magistrate bought. Wren "had wanted it found even as he hid it."
- **Value shift**: `historical curiosity → indictment`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The cipher resists, then reads as a name.
  2. The hand coming apart — nib torn, ink bled where the hand "had stopped, and held, and shaken."
  3. "Killed." — the verdict fragment; the room snaps back.
- **Exit state**: Callie understands "why someone had poisoned his tea."

### Scene 2: The revoked key
- **Setting**: The archive door, next morning; then the town hall steps; the square.
- **Characters**: Callie, the county clerk, Augustus Ralston, the square's faces.
- **Purpose**: Institutional force arrives — council decision "pending the investigation," effective yesterday; Ralston's timed consolation ("Give it a week"); the leash understood (scanner, climate control, goodwill); the town's sliding eyes; a bench fragment about the Fenns.
- **Value shift**: `independence → entrapment`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The key that doesn't work; "the words of a machine."
  2. Ralston arriving "at the precise moment that looked like coincidence."
  3. The bench fragment: one of the Fenns at the county records office.
- **Exit state**: The word on the square is that Eli will be brought in.

### Scene 3: Mae's kitchen
- **Setting**: Hartwell House kitchen, evening; rain restarting; chamomile, not bergamot.
- **Characters**: Callie, Mae Hartwell.
- **Purpose**: The plea — the grandmother's story told once ("the Ralstons took the land... a minister in our family found out. He died for it"); fifty years of chosen silence; "let the dead keep their secret... For everyone else"; Callie: "I can't"; "You sound like your mother."
- **Value shift**: `belonging → rupture risk`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The kitchen's warmth; Mae's hands at their busiest.
  2. The story, flat and final, no metaphor.
  3. "I can't, Mae." — and the almost-touch at the door; lavender pressed into the coat.
- **Exit state**: Callie walks home in the rain and begins to think about the Historical Society.
