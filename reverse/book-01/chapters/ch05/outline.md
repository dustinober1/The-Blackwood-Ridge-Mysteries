# Chapter 5: The Squeeze

**Act**: 2 — The Trail and the Squeeze
**Timeline anchor**: Day 6 (daytime – 8:45 pm, into the night)
**POV**: Callie Thorne
**Word target**: 2890 (actual span: 3520)

## Chapter Goal

*Retrofitted from finished prose.* Lock the case: the keystone and the register turn inference into evidence, Eli becomes an ally with an alibi and a sighting, and Ralston's threat stops being subtext.

## Dramatic Question

Can Callie prove what she knows — and will she survive knowing it? — Proof assembled here; the survival question carried into ch6–7.

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The killer's own house
- **Setting**: The Historical Society — gingerbread trim, Ralston plaque, collections room.
- **Characters**: Callie; Wren's keystone hand.
- **Purpose**: Locked out of the archives, she works the institution Ralston funded: finds the Wren keystone in the 1962 Ralston estate lot; decodes the original — five families (Hartwell, Tucker, Fenn, Cole, Marr), the true lines, the farewell written the night Wren fled. She copies it because "the original could disappear."
- **Value shift**: `blocked → armed`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. "She was walking into the killer's own house."
  2. The keystone's desperate hand; the farewell: "I pray God someone finds this who can do what I cannot."
  3. The transcription — evidence insurance.
- **Exit state**: She goes back downstairs and opens the visitor's register.

### Scene 2: The register
- **Setting**: The Society's front desk register.
- **Characters**: Callie; three entries and a margin note.
- **Purpose**: The sequence locks — J. Fenn (3 days before Ralston; dissolved: chasing ghosts from the other side), "A. Ralston — collection review" (8 days before the murder), "Dr. W. Pike — research" (2 days later) with Pike's pencil: "showed preliminary findings to Gus Ralston — very interested, asked to see the manuscript. Good. The book deserves daylight."
- **Value shift**: `inference → evidence`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. Fenn's entry and its dissolution.
  2. Ralston in the building he funded, eight days out.
  3. Pike's eager, blind, date-stamped death warrant.
- **Exit state**: "The sequence locked, step by step."

### Scene 3: Eli's account
- **Setting**: The Ridgeway's main floor, cataloguing day; back office still taped.
- **Characters**: Callie, Eli Townsend.
- **Purpose**: The alibi and the sighting — the borrowed paperback photographed where it sits on the wrong shelf; then, unprompted: "I saw Mr. Ralston. Coming out of the back corridor." Callie asks for his trust and promises he'll be believed.
- **Value shift**: `alone → responsible for another`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The confession of the paperback; "a small wrecked sound."
  2. The photograph in place — "Send a deputy. It is still there."
  3. "I didn't tell the sheriff... they'd think—" / "I know exactly what they'd think."
- **Exit state**: Eli steadies; Callie walks home "with the notebook pressed against her ribs."

### Scene 4: The night visit
- **Setting**: The shop, 8:45 pm; the council key; the dark back room after.
- **Characters**: Callie, Augustus Ralston.
- **Purpose**: The threat, dressed as care — the lease, the $8,000 scanner, "She knew when a thing was worth protecting," "I would so hate to see what happened to Wendell happen to someone else"; the hands finally tremble; for half a second, Silas's stare; he decides to wait.
- **Value shift**: `hidden war → declared`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The lock turning — "the first true thing he had shown her."
  2. The architecture underneath when the warmth is gone.
  3. The tremor seen and acknowledged; "Think about what I've said."
- **Exit state**: Callie sits armed with the notebook in the dark, afraid all night — "she did not once stop being afraid."
