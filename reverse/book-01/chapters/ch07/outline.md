- **Setting**: The sheriff's office (Callie waiting); Ralston Hall in retelling.
- **Characters**: Callie, Dalton Cross, deputies; Augustus Ralston (brought in 2:30 pm).
- **Purpose**: The quiet arrest — "Of course, Sheriff. Let me get my coat" — and the three-second look: bewilderment, not hatred; "a man who had been misunderstood."

### Scene 2: The breaking story
- **Setting**: Dusk search results; then the town over the following day.
- **Characters**: Callie (observer); the town in pieces — the Society, the child at the plaque, Mrs. Tucker.
- **Purpose**: The evidence lands (six pages + dropper bottle from the locked desk drawer; lab match) and the damage is itemized — Society shuttered, plaque unbolted, Mrs. Tucker motionless at her gate, the town's wariness "of people who have learned that the ground they were standing on was not what they thought it was."
- **Value shift**: `justice → wound`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. The drawer's contents; the lab match.
  2. The story tearing "the way a seam gives when you pull the thread."
  3. "Not with hatred, exactly... who blamed the surveyor."
- **Exit state**: Callie keeps the shop open. "She had nowhere else to be."

### Scene 3: Eli, Cross, Mae
- **Setting**: The shop across two afternoons and one full-dark evening.
- **Characters**: Callie, Eli Townsend, Dalton Cross, Mae Hartwell.
- **Purpose**: The settlements — Eli cleared (still hands, one nod, later seen standing upright); Cross's handshake on "the level of equality" ("grateful, in a way I'm not accustomed to being"); Mae's knock — soup held out, no absolution asked: "There's a Bible on the shelf in my parlor. My grandmother's hand is in it... I could have shown you. I didn't."
- **Value shift**: `isolation → carried love`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. Eli's nod; the upright silhouette at the auction table days later.
  2. The handshake held "for the count of two, and let go."
  3. The knock (not the bell); the record offered "because the record deserved it."
- **Exit state**: The lavender stays in the coat, unwashed; the ink stains stop being scrubbed.
