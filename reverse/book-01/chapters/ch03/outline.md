## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Poison on the square
- **Setting**: The shop, third morning; the word *poison* "in every cup of coffee on the square."
- **Characters**: Callie, a customer, Dalton Cross.
- **Purpose**: The ME's homicide ruling hardens the hunt; Cross escalates — evidence is his department, the archives visit is known, Eli is where the evidence points. Callie misses the procedural kindness in his warning.
- **Value shift**: `wariness → defiance`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. "Of course it was poison" — the meniscus already told her.
  2. Cross plain: "I cannot have an amateur muddying witness statements."
  3. The wrong man's case assembling "link by link."
- **Exit state**: She locks the shop at noon and walks, again, to the town hall.

### Scene 2: The 1840–41 margin
- **Setting**: The archives, 12:14 pm; five hours gone in a sentence.
- **Characters**: Callie; Wren's hand.
- **Purpose**: The first full decode — Latin measurement above, cipher beneath; not a number but an attestation ("I filed this contrariwise, and under constraint"); three degrees = 140 acres; Pike's pencil: "Verified against the filed copy... God, Callie — it's all of it."
- **Value shift**: `ignorance → knowledge-with-cost`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. Eleanor's glass on the page; the room narrows.
  2. The cipher refuses to parse as number — the wrong assumption — then opens as a surveyor's witness-mark.
  3. "Two voices, a hundred and eighty years apart, both frightened."
- **Exit state**: The margins are now a trail; she reads deeper.

### Scene 3: Ralston at the counter
- **Setting**: The shop, ~7:15 pm; rain stopped for the first time in three days.
- **Characters**: Callie, Augustus Ralston.
- **Purpose**: The probe — warm sourdough, "You'd tell me, Callie," "So it could be properly archived"; the hands lying "with the absolute, effortful stillness of a thing that had been commanded to be still"; Callie can't find the seam and files it.
- **Value shift**: `domestic warmth → measured threat unrecognized`
- **Key Beats**:
  1. "Gus. How many years have we known each other?" — the charm renewed.
  2. "Unfinished work... in unreliable hands" — the shape of what he's actually asking.
  3. The held breath she "had heard and could not yet read."
- **Exit state**: The bread goes to Mae's porch; Callie stands at the dark window "and did not turn her back on the square."
