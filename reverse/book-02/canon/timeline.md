finished pre-publication manuscript (2026-10-11). Story-present: **late May –
mid June 2026**, ~6 months after Book 1's arrest; the present thread runs ~2.5
weeks. The named-day skeleton coheres if ch1 falls on a Sunday (LOW
CONFIDENCE — see analysis flags). 1994 events arrive only through the cipher,
the garden, and a confession — no flashback scenes.
