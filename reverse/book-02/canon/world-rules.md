## The floriography system (the engine)

- Each botanical plate carries its Victorian meaning in Marguerite's hand, plus dates, names, and compressed private shorthand ("ten thousand graded papers worth of private telegraph" — kin to Eleanor's end-of-term code). `[text]`
- **The book** is [bible-named] *Flora's Lexicon of the Virginias* (1873), a fictional regional floriography — the title never prints in the text; adoptable for series reference. Nineteenth-century, hand-colored plates. `[bible]`
- Established vocabulary (the cipher's dictionary): rosemary/*remembrance*; rue/*sorrow, regret*; yarrow/*war* (also *cure*); basil/*hatred* (also *poverty*); sweet violet/*faithfulness*; white violet/*modesty*; aconite/*hatred*; meadowsweet/*uselessness* + *the stifling of a feeling*; water lily/*purity of heart* + *the surface and what is under it*; damask rose/*love* + *secrecy*; narcissus/*self-love*; myrtle/*love*; maidenhair fern/*sincerity, secret, magic*; peony/*shame*; hollyhock/*ambition*; columbine/*folly*; **yellow rose — "Betrayal" (ch2) [ch8's "Infidelity" is a flagged inconsistency; "Betrayal" is canonical]**. `[text]`
- The cipher's discipline: it records seeing, never accusation — a date, a place by water, a name, a wet hemline, and "the careful, deliberate, rationed refusal to go one syllable past the seeing." The final plate speaks once: "For the one who reads this. You know what I could not say. Say it." `[text]`

## The garden as second text

- The Dabney house: a low Victorian cottage on Pippin Street, boxwood hedge; the garden planted in the old language — rosemary hedge, rue, white violets, central peony bed ringed with rosemary, basil alone, maidenhair fern under the kitchen window, narcissus, myrtle, a rough stone chiseled 1994 ("not a grave — there was no grave"), and the pulled-up bed with stakes only (hollyhock, yellow rose, columbine). "The book and the ground were one text, and Marguerite had written them both for the same reader." `[text]`
- On Lauren's graduation photo (kitchen mantel, facing the room): the daily ritual of making the absent girl present. `[text]`
- The garden club dissolves the Dabney beds after the arrest, distributing cuttings — "It won't be the same code. But the flowers will grow." `[text]`

## Places and mechanisms

- **Blackwater Cove**: a bite out of Cedar Lake's eastern shore, ~40 yards across; silted, dark, still; the old dock gone (four pilings remain); willows; the town stopped swimming "a decade after Lauren Coalter vanished because the place felt wrong in a way no one had ever named." Reached by the lake road east, past the school. Site of the dive (six-twelve entry; the ring at 8:14; the remains at nine). `[text]`
- **Blackwood Ridge High**: east edge of town; 1960s brick over an older foundation; *W. Ambler, Principal* on the frosted glass. `[text]`
- **The Harlow barn / the Carmody place**: 1994 lake-path geography; the barn is Wells's 1994 alibi location. `[text]`
- **The archive rekeying**: the council recalls the archive key for a "security audit," access "pending completion," no stated end — the mechanism by which Wells slows Callie without threat. A deliberate rhyme of Book 1's ch4 lockout (different actors, same town grammar). `[text]`
- **The Foxed Page**: unchanged (bell, back room, oak table, felt rest, reading nook with two leather chairs and the green-glass-shade lamp, shop phone); the reading nook is where Mae's confession happens. `[text]`
- **The bergamot register**: "almost right... one register short" — the standing measure of unfinished grief, made Eleanor's way (water off the boil; the two-finger count, one-two-three). `[text]`
- **Callie's transportation**: she WALKS (three blocks to Pippin Street; the long way through the square). No car — series canon from Book 1's continuity fixes. ch7's driving to the cove is a flagged must-fix (rework to Cross collecting her or riding with the dive team). `[text-canon + flagged]`

## Social rules (state of the town)

- The Ralston trial's shadow: a second pillar falling "in a year, to the same woman's hand" is the town's stated dread; the garden club splits rather than aligns; the groceries (Wells's thirty years of Thursdays) are "the thing Doris could not afford to lose on the strength of a dead woman's shorthand." `[text]`
- Wells's mechanism: warmth as perimeter — he never threatens; he goes to the people who listen to him, "and the warmth had done what warmth does in a town that had just spent the winter bleeding." `[text]`
- The promise economy: Doris's "bring it to me first" and its breaking — "there was nothing she could say that would unbreak it" — is tracked as a paid debt in promises.yaml. `[text]`
