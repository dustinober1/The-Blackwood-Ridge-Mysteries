# Historical Figures — the founding fraud (series reference)

Series-level reference for Books 3+, which keep touching the 1840s fraud.
Full account: `series-bible/world.md` § The historical scandal; Book 1 text
is the authority of record.

## The principals

- **Silas Ralston (1840s):** Family patriarch; orchestrated the fraud. The
  ancestor whose crimes the Ralston fortune rests on. Not to be confused with
  **Gus Ralston** (present day, Book 1's killer — tried and imprisoned).
- **Jacob Wren:** The town surveyor; coerced into falsifying the original
  grant records, secretly encoded the true measurements in three strata —
  Latin margin shorthand, a private letter-substitution cipher, and the
  keystone volume. Fled town in fear the night he finished the farewell.
  The keystone is an **untitled clothbound volume of regional survey
  records** (Society collections room, 1962 Ralston lot) — *not* a Bible.
- **Reverend Thomas Hartwell:** Mae's ancestor; the town's minister;
  discovered the fraud and was killed for it (staged riding accident, bought
  magistrate, 1843). The Hartwells' standing loss is the family wound behind
  Mae's partial knowledge.

## The five robbed families

**Hartwell, Tucker, Fenn, Cole, Marr** (~140 acres total — text-precise,
Book 1). Descendants of these names are fair game for Books 3+ material;
the **Fenn** door is deliberately open (see story-memory open doors). New
descendant characters must not contradict the fraud's mechanics or the
restitution question left open at series level.

## Usage rules for future books

- The fraud's mechanics, scope, and keystone location are locked (Book 1 text).
- Wren's cipher has exactly three strata; do not invent a fourth.
- The restitution/legal question is an open series door — it may be advanced,
  never silently resolved.
