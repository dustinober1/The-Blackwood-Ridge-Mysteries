# Eli Townsend

## Core profile

Early twenties, lean, restless, and originally dressed to disappear. A former drifter who reached Blackwood Ridge at the last bus stop before his money ended. Anxiety once made him appear guilty; growing competence now makes his hands steadier than his voice.

## External drive

Be useful enough to keep his place at The Foxed Page and in Callie's trust.

## Internal need

Believe he belongs without apology or control. His long-arc flaw is the belief that usefulness can justify deciding how truth reaches other people.

## Wound and private debt

He expects authority to blame him and makes himself small before accusation arrives. His father's final instruction—“Call when you've figured things out”—remains unpaid. Blackwood Ridge and Callie become the first place where work feels like a possible home.

## Voice and body

- Fast, breathless speech under anxiety; reflexive apologies decrease over time.
- Collar touch replaces hoodie-string fidgeting as he becomes more deliberate.
- Goes quiet and still under direct accusation.
- Sharp mind surfaces when he forgets to be afraid.
- Hand becomes increasingly steady.

## Established skills

- Provenance noticing.
- Duplicate indexing and movement paths.
- Basic archival handling and copying.
- Water/damage mapping.
- Carbon and pressure-transfer recognition.
- Comparison grids.
- Evidence-boundary discipline.
- Honest blanks rather than guessed entries.

## Current relationships

- **Callie:** De facto apprentice. He admires her and increasingly knows how she evaluates a page.
- **Cross:** Former prime suspect; now a helpful civilian whose work must remain narrow and logged.
- **Mae:** Treats him as belonging at the table without demanding gratitude.
- **Town:** Less invisible, still sensitive to being treated as transient or replaceable.

## Appearance history

### Book 1

Scapegoated for Wendell Pike's murder; cleared through Callie's work. Began standing taller and introducing himself.

### Book 2

Flagged Marguerite Dabney's hand in estate materials. Asked Callie to teach him how handwriting means something. Saturday apprenticeship formalized.

### Book 3

Assisted with research/copies and learned to remain outside rooms where he lacked authority. Competence grew without a manipulation reveal.

### Book 4

Learned duplicate protocols, damaged-label reading, water mapping, pressure transfer, comparison grids, and evidence boundaries. His blank eighth line helped reconstruct Ruth Mallory's path. Cross told him narrow work matters because it stays narrow.

### Book 5 — visible entering state

Works part time at The Foxed Page, builds verified comparison grids, and appears helpful, loyal, and careful. He learns how Callie distinguishes current natural writing from older formal hand.

## INTERNAL long-arc state

Book 5 begins active curation. Eli relocates Alton Mercer's genuine current repair ticket from the shop's general repair drawer into a returned Mercer volume so Mae will find it during authorized sorting. He alters nothing, fabricates no guilt, and does not contaminate police evidence. He omits the relocation and tells himself he restored provenance. This action must remain hidden until the later series reveal.
