# Series Timeline

## Deep backstory

- **1780s:** Blackwood Ridge founded. Original land grants include Hartwell holdings; the Ralstons arrive later through trade and milling.
- **1840s:** Silas Ralston orchestrates land fraud. Surveyor Jacob Wren is coerced into false records and secretly encodes the true measurements in margins.
- **1843:** Reverend Thomas Hartwell discovers the fraud and is killed in a staged riding accident.
- **1840s–present:** The fraud becomes accepted history; private oral fragments survive.
- **1991, late autumn:** Nineteen-year-old Lila Mae Crowe dies after a Ridge Road hit-and-run. Sheriff Roland Tavis withholds investigative movement on information pointing toward Benedict Calder.
- **1994:** Lauren Coalter dies at Blackwater Cove after Wells Ambler pushes her from the old dock. The town accepts a runaway story. Marguerite Dabney begins the botanical cipher that will eventually expose him.
- **Approximately 1985:** Eleanor Thorne establishes The Foxed Page.
- **Approximately 1994:** Eleanor gives nine-year-old Callie the brass magnifying glass.
- **Approximately 2024:** Eleanor dies in The Foxed Page back room. Callie inherits the shop and unprocessed grief.

## Book chronology

### Book 1 — The Annotated Murder — late 2025 / early series year

- Wendell Pike is poisoned at Ridgeway Auction House.
- Callie decodes Jacob Wren's marginal record of the founding fraud and Reverend Hartwell's murder.
- Present evidence ties Gus Ralston to Pike's death.
- Gus is arrested; Eli is cleared; the town's power structure fractures.
- Callie finds the botanical-book hook.

### Book 2 — The Botanical Confession — late May to June 2026

- Marguerite Dabney's estate lot reaches The Foxed Page.
- Callie decodes the botanical cipher and identifies Wells Ambler.
- Lauren Coalter's remains and graduation ring are recovered from Blackwater Cove.
- Wells confesses and is arrested.
- Callie remains useful but not welcome.
- Mae speaks “earlier than Marguerite,” but the fracture with Callie is not healed.
- Eli's Saturday apprenticeship begins.

### Book 3 — The Challenger — autumn 2026

- Academic Vivian Larter comes to Blackwood Ridge with a challenge to Callie's Book 1 work.
- Larter is murdered during the dispute over missing/corrected historical records.
- Harlan Beech is arrested.
- Callie's reading is shown to be incomplete in part but not dishonest.
- Cross calls her contribution useful, not partnership.
- Town status shifts from pariah to grudging utility.

### Book 4 — The Archive Fire — early November 2026

- **Day 1:** Ruth Mallory calls Callie about “a shelf that lied twice.” Fire damages the Town Archives; Ruth is found dead.
- **Days 2–3:** The Foxed Page back room becomes a logged salvage table. Mae brings labor/supplies. Eli builds duplicate and damage paths. Ruth's cards identify Tavis, Crowe, B.C., and the night book.
- **Day 4:** Medical findings make Ruth a present-day murder victim. The 1991 Lila Mae Crowe record emerges. Cross formally asks Callie to continue inside explicit boundaries.
- **Days 5–6:** Mae supplies context; Tavis admits he kept the old file technically open while preventing movement; false paths narrow.
- **Day 7:** Ruth's duplicate protocol, ash/pressure transfer, brass cat charm, and Benedict Calder's guilty knowledge create an actionable present case.
- **Day 8:** Cross arrests Benedict Calder for Ruth's murder and related destruction/concealment; files the Lila Mae Crowe supplemental entry; authorizes a records-consultant arrangement subject to county approval.
- **Ending lock:** Case by case, boundaries written. Callie steps on the floorboard, places the brass glass beside damaged paper, and recognizes she will not be reading alone.

### Between Books 4 and 5 — November 2026 to January 2027

- County approves Callie's written records-consultant arrangement.
- The Town Archives enter phased recovery and remain a recent civic wound.
- Eli begins part-time work at The Foxed Page and practices reconstruction/provenance under Callie's supervision.
- Blackwood Ridge increasingly brings documentary questions to Callie.

### Book 5 — The Planted Page — January 25 to February 1, 2027

- **January 25:** Callie and Eli assess Alton Mercer's legal-manuscript collection. Callie observes his current natural handwriting. Alton confirms a next-morning estate appointment.
- **Night of January 25:** Alton leaves an 8:47 p.m. voicemail confirming a trust restatement. Nora Mercer Shaw incapacitates and smothers him, stages an overdose and forged note, removes the restatement, alters her exit time, and leaves after using her side-door code at 9:14.
- **January 26:** Tess finds Alton. Cross authorizes Callie to examine the note under a case-specific scope. Callie identifies the first repeated constructed form but requests samples.
- **January 27:** Comparison samples expose multiple suspect secrets. Mae finds a genuine current repair ticket in a returned Mercer volume; prior loose-paper location remains unestablished.
- **January 28:** Callie demonstrates simulation/transfer indicators. Medical examiner establishes homicide independently.
- **January 29:** Current trust, $2.4 million residue, threatened removal, and $286,400 audit become precise.
- **January 30:** Access ledger, side-door code, medication-return path, and false-suspect alibis narrow the case.
- **January 31:** Examiner corroboration and warrant recovery of practice sheets, graphite transfer, missing restatement, and medication envelope establish probable cause.
- **February 1:** Nora invokes counsel and is arrested without confession. Trust/foundation control is frozen. Case resolves; repair-ticket route gap remains quiet.

## Current series state after Book 5 initialization

- **Callie:** Bounded records consultant; public reputation approaching its peak; still emotionally restrained; at risk of treating usefulness as identity.
- **Cross:** Controls official procedure and trusts Callie's documentary method within written limits.
- **Mae:** Workable new relationship with Callie through permission, labor, food, labels, and practical limits—not complete emotional restoration.
- **Eli:** De facto apprentice with established reconstruction skills. Book 5 begins his active hidden curation of a genuine document path; Callie does not know.
- **Town:** Brings records to Callie because it needs her; gratitude and resentment coexist.
- **Books 6–8:** Pattern recognition, Eli reveal, and finale remain open and viable.
