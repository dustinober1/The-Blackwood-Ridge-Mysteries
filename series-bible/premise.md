# Series Premise

## Logline

In a historic Virginia valley town where old money is built on old lies,
antiquarian bookshop owner and archivist Callie Thorne uses her gift for
decoding historical marginalia — the handwritten notes left in book margins —
to solve murders that are inextricably tied to the town's buried past.

## Over-arching arc

Each book surfaces a different layer of Blackwood Ridge's history, forcing
Callie deeper into the moral tension between truth and community. The town's
founding families each carry secrets encoded in documents only Callie can read.
As the series progresses, Callie's reputation shifts from quiet shopkeeper to
the person who sees what the town has spent generations hiding — and the town's
relationship with her shifts from wariness to reliance to resentment and back.

## What binds the books

- **Callie Thorne** is the series protagonist — her emotional arc (isolation to
  connection, grief to acceptance, dead to living) carries across all books.
- **Blackwood Ridge** is the shared setting — the town itself is a character
  with layered history that each book peels back.
- **Marginalia as forensic evidence** — Callie's unique investigative lens. Each
  book features a different kind of archival/document-based mystery.
- **The cost of truth** — each book tests Callie's conviction that uncovering the
  past is worth the human price.

## Series theme

The truth versus protecting people. Is uncovering the past always the right
call? Each book tests this question from a different angle, gradually
transforming Callie from someone who hides among the dead into someone who can
honor the past while living in the present.

## Tone

Literary and atmospheric with heavy moral weight. The comforting sensory-rich
blanket of a classic cozy (old leather, rain, hot tea, bookshop stacks) with a
gothic-tinged chill underneath. Smart, atmospheric, emotionally resonant.

## Comps

1. **The Secret, Book & Scone Society** (Ellery Adams) — closest tonal cousin
2. **The Thursday Murder Club** (Richard Osman) — cousin for moral weight
3. **The Cracked Spine** (Paige Shelton) — structural blueprint for archival sleuthing

## Market lane

Smart / Atmospheric Bookish Cozy with Heart. Readers who want rich sensory
aesthetic and a clever puzzle with emotional resonance over silly tropes.
