# The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries — Full Remaster Design

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** Approved for planning  
**Scope:** Eight ebook editions; fully validated and upload-ready; no external publishing or retailer upload.

## Goal

Remaster all eight books in *The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries* under one standardized editorial, ebook-production, metadata, and cover system while preserving the series’ established canon and keeping every legacy asset recoverable.

## Current baseline

- Books 1–2 are published.
- Books 3–6 have substantially complete manuscripts and release/package artifacts, but require remaster audit and standardized rebuild.
- Book 7, *The Reckoning*, has four drafted chapters of eight; Chapters 5–8 are planned and undrafted.
- Book 8, *The Last Margin*, is planned but has no book directory yet.
- The series bible, timeline, story memory, continuity locks, and voice DNA are authoritative.

## Editorial contract

1. The canonical series bible, book bibles, mission locks, timelines, story memory, continuity locks, and voice DNA govern all changes.
2. Every book receives a developmental, continuity, mystery-fairness, prose-quality, and reader-facing-spoiler audit.
3. Revisions may improve clarity, pacing, characterization, clue fairness, continuity, grammar, mechanics, and ebook readability.
4. No revision may change a locked killer, motive, method, evidence boundary, character arc, relationship boundary, or Book 8 reservation without a written continuity decision.
5. Books 1–6 may receive substantive improvements when the audit supports them. Every change records its reason and preserves the pre-remaster baseline.
6. Books 7–8 receive full drafting and the same editorial gates as the earlier books.
7. Final series read-through verifies cumulative continuity and voice after title-level work is complete.

## Cover system

All eight covers will be redesigned as one coordinated set. Each cover will use:

- a shared series mark and author treatment;
- common title hierarchy, typography, border language, and high-resolution portrait dimensions;
- a cohesive archival/cozy-mystery palette and recurring document/reading motif;
- distinctive story-specific imagery for each title;
- title, author, and series text that remain legible at thumbnail size;
- explicit provenance and approval metadata.

Existing covers remain available as legacy assets and are never destructively overwritten without a recoverable copy.

## Ebook upload-ready definition

Each title must have a reproducible upload package containing:

- validated EPUB;
- standardized metadata;
- title, copyright, navigation, series, and author front/back matter as applicable;
- retailer description and upload worksheet;
- approved ebook cover;
- manifest and readiness report.

Validation covers EPUB structure, navigation, internal links, image constraints, metadata consistency, title/author matching, spoiler leakage, clean rendering, package naming, and manifest completeness. A final series matrix verifies all eight titles against the same fields and naming rules.

## Production boundaries

- No external upload, distribution, or publication is included.
- Synced reference material remains read-only where project instructions designate it so.
- Legacy manuscripts, accepted baselines, and covers remain recoverable through version control and provenance records.
- Book 7 may not skip its remaining mission-lock and drafting gates.
- Book 8 must be architected from the series bible and Book 7 ending state before prose drafting.

## Completion evidence

The remaster is complete only when all eight books have:

1. a final manuscript passing the editorial and continuity gates;
2. a remastered cover passing visual and technical checks;
3. a standardized EPUB that passes the repository’s validation tooling and renders cleanly;
4. complete upload metadata and package artifacts;
5. a title-level readiness report;
6. a passing row in the final series-wide audit matrix.
