# Content Notes — The Pattern

- Cozy-mystery violence: one fatal blunt-force assault, described through aftermath rather than graphic action.
- Historical concealment of an accidental death and remains recovery.
- Financial and civic corruption, family legacy pressure, plagiarism, privacy violation, and unauthorized document removal.
- No romance arc for Callie and Cross.
- No reader-facing reveal of the hidden curator.
- Atmosphere: cold spring rain, river fog, dogwood buds, old wood, map drawers, church ledgers, hardware-store brass polish, and the warm practical refuge of The Foxed Page.
