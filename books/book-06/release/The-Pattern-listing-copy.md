## Full retailer description

The maps were real. The trail through them was not.

Local historian Miriam Vale has spent months reconstructing what county surveyor June Halbrook found before she vanished in 1989: a right-of-way quietly omitted from the record, and a payment no one can quite explain. When Miriam is killed inside the Bellweather Grange map room, Sheriff Dalton Cross opens a murder investigation — and Callie Thorne notices something else entirely. Genuine historical documents, untouched for decades, now carry small pencil marks that lead precisely to the comparisons she is best equipped to make.

Working within the strict limits of her records-consultant role, Callie follows a county ledger, a missing field-book tracing, and a present-day redevelopment deal built on land whose title may not be as clean as everyone needs it to be. She can build the case for Miriam's murder. What she cannot yet prove is who has been quietly steering her through the records — or why.

*The Pattern* is Book 6 of The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries, an atmospheric cozy mystery series about old records, small-town secrets, and an amateur sleuth who is starting to wonder whether she is asking the questions, or being led to them.

## Series description

In Blackwood Ridge, Virginia, old books and public records keep the truths the town would rather smooth away. Antiquarian bookseller Callie Thorne follows marginal notes, handwriting habits, ciphers, and damaged documents through atmospheric small-town mysteries where every solved case changes what her neighbors believe about their past—and about the woman willing to read it closely.
