# Chapter 2 Mission Lock — A Fall That Did Not Fit

## Dominant function

Break the apparent-accident explanation by demonstrating that Miriam Vale's body and the rolling ladder were deliberately arranged, while preserving medical, weapon, suspect, annotation, custody, and arrest limits.

## Opening state

- **Date:** Thursday, April 15, 2027.
- **Time:** Immediately after Chapter 1's 7:39 p.m. ending.
- **Location:** Bellweather Grange map-room threshold.
- Bellweather Grange remains secured under Sheriff Dalton Cross and Deputy Bell.
- Miriam's body, rolling ladder, gray box, accession register, appointment card, room fixtures, and removable map weights remain controlled scene material.
- Callie's narrow threshold authorization has ended. She has identified only the register and appointment card and has observed the photographed brake positions.
- No complete cause or manner of death has been lawfully declared.
- Public and trustee pressure favors a tragic fall and quick reopening.
- Callie knows the ladder did not roll but does not know how Miriam died.

## Chapter objectives

1. Preserve the Chapter 1 scene, photography, transport, and custody sequence.
2. Document both manual wheel locks, caster housings, tread contact, dust relationships, floor position, ladder rail, body position, and relevant distances before movement.
3. Establish that the ladder was moved into its final position without its wheels rotating and that impact did not engage both manual locks.
4. Allow preliminary medical findings to challenge a simple ladder fall through the proper medical channel, without giving Callie forensic authority.
5. Introduce possible brass-polish transfer on Miriam's cuff with explicit non-uniqueness limits.
6. Inventory the room's ordinary brass fixtures and six removable map weights under Bell and Cross's control.
7. Establish that one map-weight handle appears recently wiped or cleaned relative to the others without identifying it as the weapon.
8. Show Tara Bellweather's understandable concern for the Grange and public pressure for an accident classification and reopening.
9. Advance Leo March as a credible early suspect through the witnessed quarrel, attribution dispute, and deleted email.
10. Preserve that Leo's message concerns research credit or plagiarism and does not establish presence or murder.
11. Have Cross challenge and reject any attempt to individualize the paired graphite bracket to a writer from its short shape.
12. End with Cross opening a homicide inquiry on cumulative documented facts, without an arrest basis.

## Success condition

By the chapter end, the reader understands:

- why a rolling-ladder fall no longer explains Miriam's position;
- that both brake tabs were manually engaged before scene movement;
- that dust, tread, and floor relationships show the ladder did not roll into the staged position;
- that Miriam's significant wound does not fit the apparent ladder contact points;
- that preliminary medical findings support a blow from a rounded heavy object while withholding weapon identity;
- that possible brass polish transferred to Miriam's cuff but could have several ordinary room sources;
- that one map-weight handle differs from the others because it appears recently wiped;
- why Leo remains a plausible suspect whose conflict is real but not yet homicidal proof;
- why the graphite bracket cannot identify a writer;
- that the death is now a homicide inquiry with no arrest basis.

## Failure conditions

The chapter fails if it:

- reveals Dana Wren as the killer;
- declares a brass map weight conclusively to be the weapon;
- supplies impossible or overly certain forensic findings;
- lets Callie diagnose the wound or identify cause or manner of death;
- lets Callie handle, move, seize, package, inventory, or test evidence;
- lets Callie independently question Tara, Leo, or another suspect;
- transfers scene, medical, interview, preservation, warrant, legal, or arrest authority from Cross;
- treats the graphite bracket as individualized handwriting proof;
- identifies Eli as curator, gives him POV, or supplies private confirmation;
- proves the murder and modern annotation share a maker or plan;
- resolves Leo's complete false path;
- introduces missing field-book sheet 47 or the full Halbrook solution;
- produces an arrest basis;
- drafts Chapter 3 prose.

2. **Public pressure and the Grange boundary:** Tara supplies maintenance/access facts and asks about reopening; trustees and callers favor a tragic fall; Cross refuses premature closure or accusation.

5. **Brass transfer and room inventory:** Bell's logged photographs show possible brass polish on Miriam's cuff, several ordinary brass sources, six removable map weights, and one differently cleaned handle; no weapon declaration.
6. **Leo pressure and the chapter hook:** A preserved cached preview shows an attribution dispute and deletion; Cross verifies timing and controls interview/preservation; he rejects graphite-shape identity; the homicide inquiry opens without arrest.

## Required ending hook

The medical re-review supports a blow from a rounded heavy object, while Bell's controlled inventory shows that one removable brass map-weight handle was newly cleaned. The map weight is not conclusively identified as the murder weapon, no suspect is arrested, and the modern annotation remains unconnected to the homicide by proof.
