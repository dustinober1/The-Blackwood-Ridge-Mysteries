# Chapter 5 Mission Lock — The Road Through Bellweather

## Canon repair applied before drafting

The Book 6 initialization package established the existence and narrative function of the 1989 payment and Dana Wren's present financing guarantee but did not lock the exact amount, record identifiers, lender, facility amount, guaranty amount, or title-condition language. It also used conflicting shorthand about whether Dana's father paid or received the 1989 money.

For Chapter 5 and all later Book 6 work, the following exact record chain controls:

### Historical payment lock

- **Date:** October 6, 1989.
- **Index source:** County Treasurer Reimbursement Index, 1989, folio 118, entry 17.
- **Voucher:** Road Fund voucher `R-1047`.
- **Warrant:** County warrant register number `4176`.
- **Account path:** `Road Fund / South District Contingency / Emergency Retrieval`.
- **Description:** `south line retrieval`.
- **Payee:** `Wren Grading Company`.
- **Amount:** `$4,860.00`.
- **Historical relationship:** The 1989 county business-registration card and the warrant endorsement identify Dana Wren's late father as Wren Grading Company's sole proprietor and receipt signatory.
- **Meaning limit:** The authenticated chain proves that the county paid Wren Grading Company under a disguised or at least unusually opaque description three days after June Halbrook's October 3 complaint. It supports a historical concealment and motive path. It does not by itself prove who removed sheet 47, what exact work the payment purchased, who knew Halbrook's complete route, how Halbrook died, or that Dana committed any present crime.

The controlling mystery solution must therefore be read as the county paying Dana's father's company in connection with the suppression, not Dana's father paying a third party.

### Present financing lock

- **Project entity:** `Bellweather Landing Redevelopment LLC`.
- **Lender:** `Blue Ridge Community Bank`.
- **Commitment identifier:** `BLR-2027-031`.
- **Commitment date:** March 29, 2027.
- **Bridge facility:** `$1,800,000`.
- **Dana Wren's limited personal guaranty:** `$600,000`, plus documented enforcement costs.
- **Closing deadline:** May 3, 2027.
- **Title condition:** Continued advances require recordable title without an undisclosed public right-of-way or materially adverse access claim across the Bellweather river parcel.
- **Consequence limit:** Restoration or enforcement of the public right-of-way would permit the lender to suspend undisbursed advances and require cure, replacement collateral, or revised closing terms. It would not automatically establish default, bankruptcy, fraud, presence at the Grange, or murder.
- **Custody:** The county development authority's project file contains a lender-certified commitment and guaranty exhibit submitted as a condition of local redevelopment participation. The authority custodian authenticates the file. Dana's signature and the lender certification establish the guaranty without relying on rumor or Mae's memory.
