# Chapter 1 Mission Lock — The Box at Closing

## Dominant function

Introduce the Book 6 thematic problem—that genuine evidence may reach Callie through a deliberately shaped route—while establishing Miriam Vale as a living, specific person, her June Halbrook research, the first modern annotation, and the murder discovery.

## Opening state

- **Date:** Thursday, April 15, 2027.
- **Time:** Approximately 4:30 p.m.
- **Location:** The Foxed Page near closing.
- Cold spring rain has recently passed.
- Callie’s public request queue is normalized. She is professionally confident and increasingly burdened by being treated as Blackwood Ridge’s default reader.
- Mae and Callie operate in their practical, permission-based new normal.
- Eli is visibly useful, competent, careful with provenance, and willing to leave unknown fields blank.

## Chapter objectives

1. Establish Callie’s post-Book 5 reputation without repeating the Book 5 opening structure.
2. Introduce Miriam as a historian with a distinctive voice, guarded incomplete work, and a life beyond clue delivery.
3. Establish Miriam’s research into June Halbrook’s 1989 disappearance without explaining the full cold-case solution.
4. Place the genuine Bellweather Grange accession register under ordinary, controlled access.
5. Introduce the paired graphite bracket as a plausible research notation.
6. Establish that the bracket crosses repair tissue installed in 2025, dating it broadly without identifying its maker.
7. Plant Miriam’s 6:15 meeting notation exactly as `D.W. — title line / six fifteen`.
8. Introduce credible professional conflict with Leo March without front-loading the suspect matrix.
9. Discover Miriam beneath the rolling ladder at approximately 7:12 p.m.
10. Preserve Cross’s scene, custody, interview, legal-conclusion, warrant, and arrest authority.
11. End with the physical inconsistency that both rolling-ladder wheels are locked.

## Success condition

By the chapter end, the reader understands:

- who Miriam is and why her loss matters;
- that she is researching June Halbrook and a missing survey/title line;
- why Callie accepts a narrow look at the accession register;
- what the paired bracket looks like and why its physical sequence requires provenance review;
- that Miriam expected a 6:15 meeting associated with `D.W.` and “title line”;
- that Miriam’s death initially resembles a fall from a rolling ladder;
- that the locked wheels make the apparent accident unstable.

## Failure conditions

The chapter fails if it:

- reveals Dana Wren as the killer;
- explains the complete Halbrook solution;
- identifies the brass map weight as the weapon;
- identifies Eli as the curator or gives him suspicious private knowledge;
- uses an Eli POV or private confirmation;
- treats the bracket as magical proof or individualized handwriting evidence;
- gives Callie uncontrolled scene access, evidence custody, interviews, or legal authority;
- turns Cross into an unrestricted partner;
- makes Mae a detective;
- front-loads all suspects or decisive later evidence;
- delays Miriam’s death discovery beyond the chapter’s effective late-middle movement;
- drafts any Chapter 2 prose.

## Knowledge and evidence boundary

### Callie may know by chapter end

- Miriam was researching June Halbrook.
- Miriam believed the paired bracket mattered.
- The bracket was made after the accession register’s 2025 repair tissue was installed, assuming the conservation record is accurate.
- The bracket’s maker, exact date, purpose, and route remain unknown.
- Miriam expected a 6:15 meeting connected to `D.W.` and “title line.”
- Miriam was found beneath a rolling ladder.
- Both ladder wheels were locked.
- A simple rolling-ladder fall does not fully fit.

### Callie must not know

- Dana killed Miriam.
- A brass map weight caused the fatal injury.
- The Halbrook right-of-way tracing was removed.
- June Halbrook’s final location.
- Dana’s financing guarantee or full family concealment.
- Who created or routed the modern marks.
- That Eli created or routed anything.

### Procedure

Cross controls the scene, emergency response, photography, evidence, witness accounts, official language, and all later legal decisions. Callie first gives a witness account, remains outside the controlled map room, and receives only a narrow threshold authorization after Bell’s initial photography. She identifies the register and appointment card; she touches nothing and makes no medical or legal conclusion.

## Locked scene architecture

1. **The Foxed Page near closing:** April rain, normalized request queue, practical Mae boundary, competent Eli provenance work, Miriam’s arrival with the gray archival box.
2. **Miriam’s request:** June Halbrook’s disappearance, incomplete theory, paired-bracket photocopy, why Miriam selected Callie, and the guarded 6:15 meeting.
3. **Bellweather Grange access:** Tara’s access log and handling rules; genuine accession register; bracket crossing 2025 repair tissue; narrow dating conclusion only.
4. **Social pressure:** Leo March’s professional conflict with Miriam; `D.W. — title line / six fifteen`; Miriam retains unfinished theory.
5. **Body discovery and response:** Tara finds Miriam at approximately 7:12 p.m.; 911, rescue, deputies, Cross, Bell, scene security, and Callie held outside.
6. **The fact that does not fit:** Under narrow authorization, Callie identifies the register and card. Bell photographs the ladder hardware. Cross confirms both wheels are locked.

## Required ending hook

The final recognition is physical and limited: the rolling ladder’s wheels were locked, so the ladder had not rolled as the apparent accident required. The chapter does not resolve Miriam’s fatal injury, the staging method, or the killer.
