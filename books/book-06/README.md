# Book 6 — The Pattern

**Author:** Vesper Blythe  
**Series:** The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries #6  
**Genre:** Atmospheric cozy mystery / amateur sleuth  
**Target:** 25,000 manuscript words across eight chapters  
**Status:** Ready to draft; no manuscript prose exists

## Controlling function

Callie recognizes that important discovery paths have been curated for her. She can prove she has been steered, but she cannot yet prove who did it.

## Case hook

Local historian Miriam Vale is murdered while researching the 1989 disappearance of county surveyor June Halbrook. Genuine historical records contain recent pencil annotations arranged for Callie to notice and follow.

## Production boundary

The present murder is fully solvable without identifying the hidden curator. Book 6 may prove steering, but it must not expose Eli Townsend, invalidate any earlier solution, or draft beyond the locked eight-chapter architecture without a chapter-specific production pass.
