## Fair-play distribution

Every suspect lies for a distinct reason: financial survival, plagiarism, family protection, workplace misconduct, or unauthorized commercialization. Only Dana combines present motive, exact opportunity, murder means, false departure, transfer evidence, and possession of the removed tracing. Tara's murder opportunity is independently closed without erasing her custody breach or trustee consequences.
