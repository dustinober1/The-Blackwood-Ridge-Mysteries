## Fair-play distribution

Every suspect lies for a distinct reason: financial survival, plagiarism, family protection, workplace misconduct, or unauthorized commercialization. Only Dana combines present motive, exact opportunity, murder means, false departure, transfer evidence, and possession of the removed tracing.
