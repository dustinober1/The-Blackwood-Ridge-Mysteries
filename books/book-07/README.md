## Controlling function

Callie Thorne deliberately creates a lawful, private, reversible false discovery to test whether someone will make an unsupported route physically true. The test exposes Eli Townsend's role in curating genuine document paths beginning in Book 5 and continuing through Book 6.

The standalone murder remains analytically and legally separate. Eli is not the killer and did not create, cause, direct, conceal, or materially assist the death.

## Case hook

Independent paper conservator and grant-compliance reviewer **Imogen Price** discovers that the Blackwood Ridge Heritage Center's celebrated **Blackwood Civic Petition Book**, represented as one intact 1912 volume, is actually a composite assembled from five genuine leaves drawn from unrelated civic campaigns. Before she can suspend the exhibition and file her report, she is killed inside the center's powered compact shelving.

The apparent equipment failure becomes homicide when controller records show a maintenance-mode closure during the death window and the safety system is found to have been deliberately bypassed.

## Locked immediate solution

- **Victim:** Imogen Price.
- **Killer:** Vivienne Croft, executive director of the Blackwood Ridge Heritage Center.
- **Motive:** Protect an $840,000 state heritage grant, a $210,000 donor match, the center's opening, and Vivienne's career from exposure of the composite artifact and her false unified-provenance certification.
- **Method:** Vivienne uses maintenance override key `MO-2` and a removable commissioning bridge to bypass the compact-shelving safety edge, then closes aisle B while Imogen is inside.
- **Death window:** Thursday, June 10, 2027, 5:43–5:51 p.m.; the controller records the decisive maintenance-mode closure from 5:46:12 to 5:48:03 p.m.
- **Staging:** Vivienne reopens the aisle, removes the bridge, trips the emergency stop, and leaves the scene to resemble a shelving malfunction. She removes Imogen's assembly worksheet and conceals it in her grant binder.
- **Arrest basis:** Mechanical/controller evidence, physical bypass evidence, documentary motive and concealment, opportunity records, and warrant recoveries. No confession is required.

## Controlled false-discovery result

Callie's test uses only shop-owned material, an explicitly unverified sandbox catalog entry, and an empty training folder. It is outside the homicide, outside police custody, outside every warrant and charging document, and incapable of proving murder or creating guilt.

Eli responds by moving a genuine shop-owned 1912 circular into the empty training folder and entering a route slip that makes the false cross-reference appear materially supported. That movement is only one corroborating fact. Eli's exposure rests on the controlled result plus independently authenticated Book 5 and Book 6 access, timing, shop logs, institutional visitor records, scanner history, movement opportunities, comparison-grid knowledge, and the existing Mercer routing slip.
