# Mystery Solution — The Reckoning

## Commercial identity

Independent paper conservator and grant-compliance reviewer **Imogen Price**, fifty-four, is killed inside the Blackwood Ridge Heritage Center while examining the institution's centerpiece artifact, the **Blackwood Civic Petition Book**.

The volume is publicly represented as one intact 1912 petition campaign. In fact, it is a composite assembled from five genuine leaves drawn from unrelated 1911–1913 civic efforts. The signatures and leaf text are authentic. The binding, sequence, catalog identity, provenance narrative, and claim of unity are false.

## Immediate killer

**Vivienne Croft**, fifty-nine, executive director of the Blackwood Ridge Heritage Center.

## Motive

Vivienne assembled the composite in 2018 after the center failed to secure a single artifact strong enough to anchor its capital campaign. She used an empty contemporary clerk's binding and five genuine orphan leaves from separate accession streams, then represented the result as an intact 1912 petition volume.

The false unified provenance later supported:

- an **$840,000 Virginia state heritage grant**;
- a **$210,000 private donor match**;
- the center's June 11 opening exhibit, *The Names That Built the Ridge*;
- Vivienne's signed certification that the object had continuous institutional provenance;
- favorable press and a pending regional accreditation review.

Imogen's draft compliance report concludes that the leaves must be separated, the exhibit halted, the grant office notified, and the catalog history audited. Exposure threatens grant suspension or clawback, donor withdrawal, the center's opening, Vivienne's position, and possible fraud review.

Vivienne believes the institution's survival justifies preserving a coherent story assembled from genuine parts. That belief mirrors the series theme without connecting her to Eli.

## Murder method

The Heritage Center's collection annex uses powered compact shelving. Normal operation requires a clear aisle, functioning photo beam, live pressure-sensitive safety edge, and no emergency-stop condition.

Vivienne has legitimate training on maintenance override key **`MO-2`**. She also retains access to a removable commissioning bridge used by the shelving contractor during testing.

On Thursday, June 10, 2027:

1. Vivienne tells Imogen that the original transfer slips for one leaf are stored in Box `B-17`.
2. Imogen enters aisle B at approximately 5:43 p.m.
3. Vivienne inserts the commissioning bridge into the safety-edge test port and turns `MO-2` to maintenance mode.
4. From end panel 2 she commands the shelving closed.
5. The moving carriage compresses Imogen against the fixed end panel. The fatal event remains off-page.
6. The controller records a maintenance-mode closure from **5:46:12 to 5:48:03 p.m.**, including an abnormal current rise and manual reopen.
7. Vivienne reopens the aisle, removes the bridge, trips the emergency stop, and leaves the shelving partly open to suggest equipment failure.
8. She takes Imogen's assembly worksheet and conceals it inside her grant binder.

- **Medical conclusion:** death occurred during the controller-recorded compression event, within the locked **5:43–5:51 p.m.** interval.

## Staging and concealment

Vivienne relies on a known commissioning defect to make the death look like a tragic equipment failure. She:

- restores the system from maintenance to ordinary mode;
- removes the commissioning bridge;
- trips the emergency stop;
- leaves the aisle partly open;
- returns `MO-2` to the director's key cabinet;
- removes Imogen's worksheet;
- alters the handwritten key-check line from `5:50` to `4:50`;
- proceeds to the Heritage Center opening reception and claims she left before 5:35.

The nonvolatile controller log survives. A transparent archival-polyester sliver remains in the safety-edge channel. The altered key line, service-door credential, parking time, and controller event preserve the opportunity sequence.

## Access and opportunity

- controls the director's key cabinet;
- signed the shelving maintenance-training receipt;
- possesses the override code and knows the commissioning bridge;
- has unrestricted administrative access to the restricted corridor;
- knows Imogen's Box `B-17` review plan;
- has an unexplained interval between her claimed departure and her first independently verified reception appearance;
- uses the service exit at 5:51 and enters the municipal parking deck at 5:58.

The seven-minute drive and walk to the reception are ordinary. Her first continuous witness period begins at 6:06.

## Physical and mechanical proof

1. **Controller log:** Nonvolatile data records a maintenance-mode closure, safety-input bypass, abnormal current rise, and manual reopen from end panel 2 during the death window.
2. **Safety channel:** A transparent archival-polyester sliver remains inside the test channel. It is not an ordinary shelving component.
3. **Physical fit:** Under warrant, a cut exhibition-mount sheet in Vivienne's private preparation drawer has an edge and missing corner that physically fit the recovered sliver. The fit is documented by the examiner; the material class alone is not unique.
4. **Commissioning bridge:** The bridge body is recovered from Vivienne's locked grant tote with residue from the same mount adhesive class. Residue is supportive, not independently identifying.
5. **Medical/scene relationship:** Imogen's injuries and transfer to the fixed end-panel felt fit powered compression, not a fall, collapse, or spontaneous shelf drift.
6. **System condition:** Independent testing shows the safety edge stops the shelves unless the maintenance bridge and override are used together.

## Documentary proof

1. Imogen's voice note and calendar establish the Box `B-17` review.
2. The five leaves carry separate historical accession numbers, incompatible paper formats, different sewing-hole positions, and source-box edge patterns.
3. Earlier institution photographs show the leaves separate before the composite binding was created.
4. Vivienne's signed grant certification claims continuous unified provenance and an intact 1912 volume.
5. Imogen's recovered assembly worksheet identifies all five source streams and states `STOP EXHIBIT / NOTIFY GRANT OFFICE`.
6. The worksheet is recovered inside Vivienne's grant binder, where it had no authorized reason to be.
7. The handwritten `MO-2` checkout line was altered from `5:50` to `4:50`; paper indentation and the original digital key-cabinet alert preserve the later time without relying on handwriting identity.
8. Contractor records show Vivienne completed override training and received the warning that maintenance mode defeats ordinary safety interlocks.
