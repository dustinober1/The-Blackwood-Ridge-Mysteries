# Mercer Preservation Foundation Office

## Location

Restored freight depot near the old rail spur, five minutes from the square. Public gallery occupies the former waiting room; staff offices sit behind glass partitions installed to preserve the original brick.

## Story function

- Displays Nora's real competence and civic value.
- Houses restricted-grant records, split invoices, board minutes, and current project files.
- Gives Gideon a legitimate work connection without making him the murderer.
- Becomes the target of a late warrant search.

## Nora's office

Orderly without looking sterile. Project binders are color-coded. Alton's old bench-note photocopies sit inside a foundation-history folder. Practice sheets, graphite transfer, the missing restatement, and medication-return envelope are concealed among legitimate working papers rather than in a theatrical secret compartment.

## Atmosphere

Radiator heat, cold brick, printer toner, rolled plans, and coffee left too long on a warming plate. The foundation is not fake. Real preservation work continues around the crime, which makes Nora's moral self-justification credible.
