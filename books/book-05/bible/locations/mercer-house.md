# Mercer House

## Function

Primary crime scene, family-pressure setting, manuscript collection site, and physical expression of Alton Mercer's controlled stewardship.

## Exterior

Red brick, dark winter ivy, white-painted stone trim, a circular drive, side library entrance, and a gate cottage beyond clipped yews. Snow remains in the shaded curve beside the house after the square has gone dry.

## Interior feel

Nothing is dusty, but little feels touched without permission. Rooms carry waxed wood, old paper, banked fireplaces, and the faint medicinal sweetness of Alton's evening brandy. Labels are discreet and expensive. Family photographs are placed like exhibits.

## Study

- Large walnut desk facing inward rather than toward windows.
- Green leather blotter and matching appointment pad.
- Reading chair near a low table.
- Settee with two embroidered cushions; one is used in the murder and returned rotated.
- Red portfolio normally holding the trust restatement.
- Locked medication-return box in adjacent cabinet, accessible to Tess and Nora as healthcare proxy.
- Fountain pen tray and cream Mercer stationery.
- Side door visible through library passage but not from main hall.

## Legal-manuscript library

- Insured collection; handling visitors recorded.
- Bench notebooks arranged by term and year.
- Current catalog cards Alton updates himself.
- Caleb's tracing tissue, photography scales, and catalog equipment.
- Returned chancery volume later transported to The Foxed Page under documented shop work.

## Continuity rules

- Do not turn the house into a gothic mansion or secret-passage puzzle.
- Access questions are ordinary: keys, codes, ledgers, staff habits, family privilege.
- The crime remains domestic and documentary, not architectural spectacle.
