# World Additions — The Planted Page

## Season and atmosphere

Late January into early February 2027. Old snow survives in north-facing gutters and hedge shadows. The square is bright with winter glare rather than holiday warmth. The Town Archives are still under phased restoration, and every temporary box or closed room reminds Blackwood Ridge that its records remain vulnerable.

## Public temperature

Callie's status has changed from grudging utility to reluctant civic dependence. People leave envelopes at The Foxed Page, ask her to verify family letters, and invoke her name in disputes she has not agreed to enter. The attention carries gratitude, entitlement, resentment, and fear. The town has not become uniformly welcoming.

## Mercer House

A late-nineteenth-century brick estate on the north edge of town, enlarged in controlled stages and preserved so carefully that comfort feels curated. Important spaces:

- **Private study:** Alton's writing desk, reading chair, settee, red trust portfolio, fountain pens, appointment pad, and current working papers.
- **Legal-manuscript library:** Bench notebooks, annotated legal volumes, catalog cards, locked cases, and the collection Callie is asked to assess.
- **Side library entrance:** Family/foundation access point with personal codes.
- **Main hall access ledger:** Tess records visitors handling insured manuscripts.
- **Gate cottage:** Tess's home and a concrete stake in the current trust.
- **Winter garden:** Used for a varied suspect conversation and an image of preservation under glass.

## Mercer Preservation Foundation

Located in the restored freight depot near the old rail spur. The foundation funds local preservation, holds restricted grants, and maintains project files that look clean until split invoices are aligned. Nora's office contains legitimate work, family history, and the hidden practice materials recovered under warrant.

## The Foxed Page

- The front counter accumulates public requests.
- The back room can hold working comparison copies and authenticated shop records but not unlogged police evidence.
- The general repair drawer is the original location of Alton's current repair ticket.
- The returned chancery volume becomes the apparent discovery container for the planted page.
- Mae's `NOT TODAY` label creates a practical limit without changing the shop into a cheerful public office.

## Sheriff's Office

Cross uses a records-room table for comparison sessions and the final controlled interview. Bell photographs arrangements before movement. Callie's consultant authorization is stored with each case file rather than treated as permanent unrestricted access.

## Procedure additions

- Case-specific consultant memo defines scope, handling limits, confidentiality, and termination.
- Comparison samples require source, date, writing purpose, custody, and authentication.
- Callie's observations are logged as observations; Cross and the document examiner determine how they enter affidavits.
- Medical cause, trust records, access logs, and search-warrant returns remain independent proof streams.
