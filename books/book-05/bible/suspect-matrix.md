# Suspect Matrix — The Planted Page

## Fair-play requirement

## Supporting non-suspect witnesses

### Daniel Pruitt — estate attorney

- Prepared the unsigned restatement.
- Receives Alton's 8:47 p.m. voicemail.
- Authenticates trust terms, appointment, and pressure-image source.
- Does not disclose privileged strategy casually; Cross obtains what is legally available through estate authority and warrant process.

### Dr. Helen Arledge — county medical examiner

- Determines the sedative level was incapacitating but not fatal.
- Identifies smothering as cause of death in restrained, non-graphic terms.
- Keeps the murder turn independent of Callie's handwriting conclusion.

### Deputy Bell

- Photographs note, comparisons, sample positions, scene, and consultant observations.
- Preserves portable chain-of-custody and prevents Callie's reading from becoming an unrecorded private certainty.

## Suspect-order discipline

1. **Surface:** apparent suicide; family conflict is treated as aftermath rather than murder.
2. **Early pressure:** Claire and Tess because of access, missing letters, and medication handling.
3. **Handwriting pressure:** Caleb because he owns transfer tools and knows the formal hand.
4. **Financial pressure:** Gideon and Nora as split invoices emerge.
5. **Final narrowing:** Nora alone has the current trust benefit, removal threat, 9:14 access, medication path, and practice materials.

## False-solution safeguards

- Claire's theft cannot explain medical cause, note construction, or late access.
- Tess's medication irregularity identifies the staged bottle but does not place her in the study during death.
- Caleb's legitimate tracing tools create fair suspicion, but his samples and timeline do not match the forged note.
- Gideon's invoices establish motive and complicity, but his alibi is independently documented.
- Nora is not made obvious solely because she benefits. The exact restatement and audit are revealed at midpoint-plus, and decisive practice/access evidence arrives late.
