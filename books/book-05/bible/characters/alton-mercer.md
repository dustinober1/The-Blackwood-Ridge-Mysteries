# Alton Pierce Mercer

## Profile

- **Age:** 77
- **Former occupation:** Virginia circuit court judge
- **Current roles:** Founder/chair emeritus, Mercer Preservation Foundation; owner of Mercer House; legal-manuscript collector
- **Public face:** Exacting, incorruptible, generous, institutionally serious
- **Private flaw:** Makes money and access conditional; confuses stewardship with ownership; keeps people in moral debt

## Family

- Daughter: Claire Mercer Duvall
- Niece/quasi-daughter: Nora Mercer Shaw
- Niece's husband: Gideon Shaw
- House manager: Tess Rooke
- Collection consultant: Caleb Voss
- Estate attorney: Daniel Pruitt

## Handwriting canon

- Uses blue-black fountain-pen ink.
- Current pressure fades across long lines because of arthritis and grip fatigue.
- Natural `g` descenders vary.
- Writes `th`, `nd`, and `ri` joins continuously.
- Financial notes use exact account names, dates, and amounts.
- Older bench notebooks show a narrower, more formal hand that Nora copies.

## Final-day behavior

- Asks Callie to assess manuscript-transfer papers.
- Writes catalog cards and a repair ticket naturally in front of Callie and Eli.
- Protects the red portfolio.
- Leaves an 8:47 p.m. voicemail confirming a 9:00 a.m. restatement appointment.

## Death

Killed by Nora through smothering after incapacitating zolpidem. Scene staged as intentional overdose with a forged note.
