# Eli Townsend — Book 5

## State entering

Eli works part time at The Foxed Page and is Callie's de facto apprentice. His Book 4 skills—duplicate paths, indexing, pressure traces, damaged-record handling, and evidence boundaries—are established.

## External desire

Help Callie build a trustworthy handwriting-comparison set and prove he can handle increasingly exact work without overreaching.

## Internal need

Believe he can belong without controlling how useful truth reaches the people he trusts. He does not yet understand that his fear of exclusion is becoming a reason to manipulate routes.

## Visible Book 5 function

- Builds source/custody/currentness grids.
- Distinguishes older formal samples from current natural writing.
- Handles shop paperwork and returned volumes within Callie's instructions.
- Marks uncertainty and provenance gaps rather than filling them.
- Learns Callie's comparison habits and Cross's requirements for verified samples.
- Remains apparently loyal, careful, and modest.

## Visible arc

Eli grows from a skilled helper into an apprentice trusted with method, not merely supplies. Cross continues to keep him outside official investigative authority. Callie increasingly assumes he understands not only the rules but their purpose.
