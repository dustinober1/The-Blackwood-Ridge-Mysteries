# Mae Hartwell — Book 5

## State entering

Mae and Callie can work together without pretending the old harm is gone. Mae asks permission, offers labor, and does not use food or warmth as leverage.

## External desire

Help Callie navigate the Mercer family's civic context and keep The Foxed Page from becoming an unbounded public records desk.

## Internal need

Continue offering care without managing Callie's feelings, reputation, or choices.

## Book 5 function

- Knows Alton's conditional generosity and the difference between preserved buildings and controlled people.
- Provides family/civic context, not murder proof.
- Participates in authorized comparison sorting.

## Arc
