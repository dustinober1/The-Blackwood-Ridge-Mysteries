# Sheriff Dalton Cross — Book 5

## State entering

Cross has formalized Callie's role as a records consultant, case by case, with written boundaries. Book 4 proved that her method belongs in the official room when the room is controlled properly.

## External desire

Determine Alton Mercer's manner of death, protect a prominent scene from civic pressure, and build a legally sustainable case if the note is forged.

## Internal need

Trust specialized interpretation without outsourcing judgment. Cross must translate Callie's observations into medical, financial, access, examiner, and warrant steps while remaining responsible for the result.

## Book 5 function

- Issues and closes a case-specific consultant authorization.
- Requires sample authentication, source, date, purpose, and custody.
- Logs Callie's statements as observations until corroborated.
- Challenges her when shared language is treated as authorship.
- Independently obtains medical findings, trust records, access logs, subpoenas, warrants, and arrest authority.
- Protects Nora's right to counsel and does not demand a confession.

## Arc

Cross begins comfortable with the written arrangement but aware that Alton's prominence will test it publicly. He learns the role works best when he neither minimizes Callie's expertise nor treats it as a shortcut. He ends with a case made stronger because every interpretive sentence has an independent procedural path.

## Tells

- Neck rub only when genuinely out of depth, not as routine business.
- Jaw set when Alton's judicial authority or Nora's civic usefulness pressures duty.
- Hat removed in records rooms, homes of the dead, and The Foxed Page when respect matters.
- Shorter sentences as the warrant and arrest approach.

## Relationship movement

Professional warmth remains controlled. No romance, banter-heavy co-detective behavior, unrestricted access, or casual evidence sharing.
