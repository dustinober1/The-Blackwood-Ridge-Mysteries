# Nora Mercer Shaw

## Profile

- **Age:** 44
- **Role:** Executive director, Mercer Preservation Foundation
- **Relationship to Alton:** Niece, partly raised by him, quasi-daughter, healthcare proxy, successor trustee, residual beneficiary
- **Public face:** Competent, controlled, hardworking, persuasive without being flamboyant
- **Private wound:** Believes usefulness is the only secure basis for belonging

## Motive

Alton prepares to remove Nora from the trust and foundation, order an independent audit, and expose $286,400 in split invoices. His death before signature preserves her approximate $2.4 million residue and immediate control of records/accounts.

## Murder capability

- Family key and personal side-door code.
- Access to medication-return box.
- Knowledge of study, pen, stationery, bench notebooks, checks, and trust records.
- Six weeks to practice the formal hand.

## Crime sequence

- Meets Alton in study.
- Dissolves six 5 mg zolpidem tablets in brandy.
- Smothers him after incapacitation.
- Stages expired bottle and forged note.
- Removes red portfolio.
- Backdates access-ledger exit.
- Uses side-door code at 9:14.
- Conceals practice materials, transfer sheet, restatement, and medication envelope in foundation office.

## Mistakes

- Simulates shapes rather than living rhythm.
- Uses an older formal hand.
- Repeats constructed forms.
- Leaves pen lifts, graphite, and baseline traces.
- Names the foundation imprecisely.
- Backdates ledger against electronic access.
- Keeps preparation and removed records near legitimate work.

## Emotional logic

Nora believes Alton would destroy real preservation work to preserve his moral authority. She frames her act to herself as protecting the institution, staff, husband, and legacy he taught her to serve.

## Ending behavior

She does not confess. She challenges the moral interpretation, reveals limited guilty knowledge, then invokes counsel. Cross arrests on the evidence.
