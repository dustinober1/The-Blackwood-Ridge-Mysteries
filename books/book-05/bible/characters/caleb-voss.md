# Caleb Voss

## Profile

- **Age:** 38
- **Occupation:** Antiquarian dealer and Mercer legal-manuscript curator
- **Relationship to Alton:** Paid collection consultant with a purchase option on selected duplicates
- **Public face:** Charming, technically fluent, defensive about proprietary catalog methods

## Motive and opportunity

Caleb owes money on an inventory advance and fears Alton will expose a provenance substitution. He works in the study until shortly before the murder window and owns tracing tissue, graphite transfer, and high-resolution samples.

## Lie

He removes a disputed folio and denies taking it. The folio contains a lower-value substitution.

## Suspicion / clearance

His tools and sample access make him the strongest false forger. Courier scan and shop camera place him away during the death window. His transfer sheets do not contain the note language or signature model.

## Thematic function

Separates legitimate copying, catalog reproduction, and document fraud from the more intimate act of manufacturing a person's hand.
