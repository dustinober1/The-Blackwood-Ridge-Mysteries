# Tess Rooke

## Profile

- **Age:** 61
- **Occupation:** Mercer House manager
- **Relationship to Alton:** Employee and daily companion of twenty-eight years
- **Stake:** $75,000 bequest and lifetime occupancy of gate cottage under current trust
- **Public face:** Dry, discreet, unimpressed by family rank

## Function

- Maintains house-access and medication-return ledgers.
- Delivers Alton's evening tray.
- Finds the body at 8:05 a.m.
- Provides current household-writing authentication.

## Lie

Tess hides Alton's minor memory lapses and admits moving medications without logging one prior incident because she feared family intervention and loss of place.

## Suspicion / clearance

Medication access and discovery make her plausible. Her ledgers ultimately identify the expired bottle and Nora's access. Exterior record places Tess at the gate cottage before the death window.

## Thematic function

Loyalty can distort a record without becoming murder. Care and concealment are not the same but can share a doorway.
