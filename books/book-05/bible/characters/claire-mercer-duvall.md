# Claire Mercer Duvall

## Profile

- **Age:** 49
- **Occupation:** Architectural historian in Richmond
- **Relationship to Alton:** Estranged daughter
- **Public face:** Controlled, educated, openly critical of her father's foundation
- **Private pressure:** Financial strain after a failed partnership; anger that family history has been converted into institutional property

## Motive and opportunity

Current trust gives Claire $300,000 and her mother's sealed correspondence but transfers Mercer House and collection to the foundation. She argues with Alton on January 25 and has access to old family letters and the study.

## Lie

Claire removes her mother's sealed correspondence without authorization and lies about her departure time to protect the theft.

## Suspicion / clearance

Missing letters and inheritance anger point toward her. A ride receipt and bookstore camera place her away before the critical death window. Recovered letters contain no practice or staging evidence.

## Thematic function

Shows that preserving a family's papers can also become dispossession. Her misconduct is real without being murder.
