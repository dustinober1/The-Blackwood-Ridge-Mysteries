# Gideon Shaw

## Profile

- **Age:** 47
- **Occupation:** Restoration contractor
- **Relationship to Alton:** Nora's husband; foundation vendor
- **Public face:** Practical, blunt, proud of real preservation work
- **Private pressure:** Cost overruns, debt, and invoice descriptions Nora manipulated to keep projects alive

## Motive and opportunity

Alton's audit threatens Gideon's company and marriage. He knows Mercer House access routes and has extensive correction memos in Alton's hand.

## Lie

He minimizes overruns and pressures a foreman to revise invoice descriptions after the fact.

## Suspicion / clearance

Foundation payments create motive. Crew timecards, fuel receipt, and witnesses place him at a county work site during the murder window. He did not write the note or know Nora's full plan.

## Thematic function

Represents real work compromised by soft records and the temptation to treat useful outcomes as moral clearance.
