# Book 5 New Cast

## Alton Pierce Mercer — victim

Retired circuit judge, preservation patron, legal-manuscript collector, and owner of Mercer House. Publicly exacting and generous; privately controlling and conditional. His current hand is physically fatigued but precise.

## Nora Mercer Shaw — killer

Alton's niece, foundation executive director, successor trustee, residual beneficiary, and healthcare proxy. Competent and apparently devoted. Kills to preserve control, inheritance, and the institution she equates with her own worth.

## Claire Mercer Duvall — suspect

Alton's estranged daughter and architectural historian. Removes her mother's letters and lies about it. Represents family history treated as institutional property.

## Gideon Shaw — suspect

Nora's husband and restoration contractor. Financially implicated in split invoices, but physically absent during the murder window.

## Tess Rooke — suspect / discoverer

Longtime house manager and gate-cottage resident. Finds Alton, maintains access/medication ledgers, and hides benign memory lapses.

## Caleb Voss — suspect

Antiquarian dealer and collection curator. Owns tracing/transfer tools and hides a provenance substitution, creating the strongest false authorship path.

## Daniel Pruitt — estate attorney

Prepared the unsigned restatement and receives Alton's final voicemail. Authenticates the financial mechanism and missing-document path.

## Dr. Helen Arledge — county medical examiner

Establishes that the sedative level was nonfatal and that smothering caused death. Keeps homicide proof independent of handwriting interpretation.
