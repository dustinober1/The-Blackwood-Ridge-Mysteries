# Timeline — The Planted Page

## Deep backstory

- **1999–2007:** Judge Alton Mercer keeps handwritten bench notebooks in his formal public hand. Nora later uses these as primary simulation models.
- **2003:** Nora, orphaned in late adolescence, begins spending substantial time at Mercer House. Alton becomes patron, guardian figure, and gatekeeper of her education and work.
- **2014:** Nora becomes executive director of the Mercer Preservation Foundation.
- **2021:** Alton executes the current Mercer Revocable Trust. Nora is successor trustee and residual beneficiary; Mercer House and the legal-manuscript collection pass to the foundation.
- **2025–2026:** Gideon Shaw's company completes emergency preservation work with growing overruns. Nora begins splitting invoices and moving restricted foundation funds.
- **September 2026:** Total questionable transfers reach $286,400. Alton begins a private invoice review.
- **December 2026:** Alton asks estate attorney Daniel Pruitt to prepare a trust restatement removing Nora, appointing an independent trustee, ordering an audit, and redirecting the residue to the foundation.
- **Mid-December 2026–January 2027:** Nora studies Alton's old bench notebooks, annual letters, and current signatures; she practices simulating his formal hand.

## Immediate series lead-in

- **Early November 2026:** Book 4 resolves. Benedict Calder is arrested, Cross files the Lila Mae Crowe supplement, and the formal records-consultant arrangement is placed into county approval.
- **November 2026–January 2027:** Callie assists with limited archive-recovery questions under written authorization. Eli works part time at The Foxed Page and becomes increasingly competent at indexing and provenance.
- **Late January 2027:** Blackwood Ridge increasingly brings private records and civic disputes to Callie, creating a visible queue at The Foxed Page.

## Book 5 present timeline

### Monday, January 25, 2027 — Chapter 1

- Callie opens The Foxed Page to find document requests waiting at the door.
- Alton Mercer summons Callie and Eli to Mercer House to assess handwritten legal materials for foundation transfer.
- Callie observes Alton's current natural hand on catalog cards and a shop repair ticket.
- Family/foundation tensions surface among Claire, Nora, Gideon, Tess, and Caleb.
- Alton confirms a private 9:00 a.m. appointment for the next day but does not disclose its terms.
- At 8:47 p.m., Alton leaves Daniel Pruitt a voicemail confirming the trust-restatement meeting.
- Nora enters the study, administers zolpidem, kills Alton, stages the note and bottle, removes the red portfolio, backdates her access exit, and leaves through the side door after using her code at 9:14 p.m.

### Tuesday, January 26 — Chapter 2

- 8:05 a.m.: Tess discovers Alton in the study.
- Apparent scene: overdose, empty expired bottle, brandy, handwritten note, no forced entry.
- Cross treats the death as provisionally self-inflicted but preserves the scene.
- Callie receives a written case-specific consultation scope limited to document observation and comparison.
- Callie notices the repeated lowercase `g` and withholds a final conclusion.
- Town pressure forms around rapid closure and Alton's civic reputation.

### Wednesday, January 27 — Chapter 3

- Verified comparison samples are collected from Mercer House, the foundation, Pruitt, and The Foxed Page.
- Claire's unauthorized removal of her mother's letters, Tess's medication handling, Caleb's disputed folio, and Gideon's invoices create distinct false paths.
- Eli builds a source/custody/currentness comparison grid.
- A hidden route event occurs; full continuity is restricted to `eli-hidden-chronology.md`.
- Reader-visible sequence: Mae finds the genuine repair ticket during an authorized shop sort and gives it to Callie.

### Thursday, January 28 — Chapter 4

- Callie demonstrates repeated constructed forms, internal pen lifts, graphite traces, pressure mismatch, and language-habit divergence.
- The current repair ticket establishes Alton's recent natural rhythm and grip fatigue.
- State document examiner receives images and samples.
- Medical examiner reports that zolpidem was incapacitating but not fatal and that the death resulted from smothering.
- Case formally becomes homicide.

### Friday, January 29 — Chapter 5

- Daniel Pruitt discloses and authenticates the unsigned restatement and 8:47 voicemail.
- Current trust, residue, successor-trustee power, and audit consequences become precise.
- Foundation records identify $286,400 in split invoices.
- Nora, Claire, Gideon, Tess, and Caleb each move in the suspect frame.
- Cross stops Callie from treating financial benefit as authorship proof.

### Saturday, January 30 — Chapter 6

- Timeline and access reconstruction at Mercer House.
- Side-door record conflicts with Nora's handwritten exit time.
- Medication-return ledger identifies the expired bottle used in staging.
- Caleb's tracing tools create a strong false solution; Claire's letters and Tess's omissions are reframed.
- Mae places `NOT TODAY` on part of Callie's request stack, naming the cost without a speech.
- Cross establishes enough cause to seek targeted warrants but not yet arrest.

### Sunday, January 31 — Chapter 7

- State examiner corroborates simulated body text and transferred signature.
- Pressure image on Alton's appointment pad reconstructs the removed restatement heading and Nora-removal language.
- Financial subpoenas and access records converge.
- Warrant search of Nora's foundation office recovers practice sheets, graphite transfer, marked bench-note copies, the missing restatement, and medication-return envelope.
- Cross completes the probable-cause affidavit and obtains the arrest warrant.

### Monday, February 1 — Chapter 8

- Cross conducts a controlled interview with Nora. Callie explains only the portable document comparison when asked.
- Nora does not confess and invokes counsel.
- Cross arrests her on the cumulative evidence.
- Trust administration and foundation control are frozen and transferred temporarily to independent supervision.
- Callie completes the repair-ticket provenance statement; exact prior loose-paper location remains unestablished.
- The Foxed Page request queue grows. Mae's `NOT TODAY` label stays.
