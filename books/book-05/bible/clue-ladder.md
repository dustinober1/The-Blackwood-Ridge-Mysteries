## Forgery proof chain

The forgery conclusion requires the combined sequence below. No single “wrong feeling” is enough.

1. Repeated identical constructed form.
2. Verified current samples showing natural variation and pressure fatigue.
3. Internal pen lifts and ink pools where genuine joins are continuous.
4. Erased baseline and selective artificial tremor.
5. Graphite/line-start evidence around signature.
6. Language and record-purpose mismatch.
7. State examiner corroboration.
8. Warrant recovery of practice and transfer materials.

## Arrest proof synthesis

- **Murder:** Medical evidence establishes smothering after nonfatal incapacitation.
- **Staging:** Old bottle, wiped objects, forged note, and removed portfolio.
- **Motive/benefit:** Current trust, $2.4M residue, successor control, threatened removal, and audit exposure.
- **Opportunity:** Altered access ledger and 9:14 side-door code.
- **Authorship:** Practice sheets, marked models, graphite transfer, exact phrases.
- **Evidence removal:** Missing restatement recovered from Nora's office.
- **Procedure:** Cross obtains examiner review, financial records, warrants, and counsel-respecting arrest.
