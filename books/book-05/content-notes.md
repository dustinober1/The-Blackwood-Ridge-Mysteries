# Content Notes — The Planted Page

> Planning-stage production note. This is not reader-facing copy unless the author later chooses to adapt it for retailer metadata.

## Concise advisory

Off-page murder staged as suicide; discussion of sedative misuse and non-graphic smothering; grief, financial coercion, inheritance conflict, and family betrayal. No graphic violence, sexual content, or profanity.

## Treatment constraints

- The apparent suicide is treated with restraint and never romanticized.
- Alton Mercer's fears about age and loss of control may make the staging credible, but the book must not equate illness, tremor, or aging with suicidality.
- The actual killing occurs off-page. Medical findings are summarized in portable investigative terms, not described graphically.
- The forged note is analyzed through demonstrable document features, not intuition or mystical certainty.
- Nora Mercer Shaw does not confess during the arrest sequence.
- Eli's hidden action must not be surfaced in reader-facing drafting notes or prose as intentional manipulation.

## Explicit exclusions

- No supernatural explanation.
- No serial-killer escalation.
- No broad conspiracy.
- No romance-forward subplot.
- No thriller-style physical pursuit or armed confrontation.
- No Chapter 1 prose in the initialization package.
