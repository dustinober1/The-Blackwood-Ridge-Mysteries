## Full retailer description

Everyone recognizes the handwriting. That is what makes the note dangerous.

Retired circuit judge and preservation patron Alton Mercer is found dead in his private study, with a handwritten statement accepting blame for financial irregularities and asking that his trust pass without public scrutiny. Blackwood Ridge is ready to believe the apparent suicide—and ready to avoid another civic scandal.

Callie Thorne is not. Newly working as a case-by-case records consultant, the antiquarian bookseller sees that Mercer’s familiar letters do not move like his current hand. The repeated forms are too consistent, the pressure too controlled, the signature too carefully made. Someone studied the judge’s writing and built a version of him on the page.

With Sheriff Dalton Cross holding the procedural line, Callie follows verified samples, disputed records, family papers, and a missing estate document through a household where preservation has always come with conditions. To uncover the truth, she must show not only that the note was simulated—but why Blackwood Ridge wanted so badly to believe it.

*The Planted Page* is Book 5 of The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries, an atmospheric cozy mystery series about old books, documentary secrets, and an amateur sleuth who reads what others overlook.

Antiquarian bookseller Callie Thorne follows marginal notes, handwriting habits, ciphers, and damaged documents through atmospheric small-town mysteries where every solved case changes what her neighbors believe about their past—and about the woman willing to read it closely.
