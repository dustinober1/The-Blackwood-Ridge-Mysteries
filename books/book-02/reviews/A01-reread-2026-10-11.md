## Verdict on the central question

**Reads human.** The audit now returns a human writer with a house style, not a machine's polish: the families that remain are spaced, the duplicated sentences are gone, and no chapter ending is stamped. My public line changes from "ambiguous" to this: someone took the notes and did the work. Four stars, and the fifth was on the table if the em-dashes weren't the voice — which I now believe they are.
