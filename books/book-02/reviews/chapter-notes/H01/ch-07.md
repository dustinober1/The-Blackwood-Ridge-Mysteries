# H01 — Book 2 Ch 7: Blackwater Cove (lines 818–952)
**Running impression:** 3/5 · **DNF risk:** low

## Gut reaction (in Nigel's voice)
This is the payoff chapter — the cove, the ring, the remains, Wells' confession. It's handled with restraint for the most part, which I respect given the content warnings. The prose doesn't wallow in the recovery; it keeps its gaze steady. But the confession scene (911–935) is long, and the narrator keeps describing the "open palm" and shaking hands again in a way that verges on overplaying the motif.

## What worked (cite chapter + line numbers — begrudgingly, if at all)
- Atmosphere: cove description (828) captures oppressive stillness effectively; "like rotted teeth" (828) is apt without being grotesque.
- Tension in search: the waiting (834–841) builds dread; the ring moment (844–851) lands with weight.
- Restraint in recovery: (854–855) avoids graphic detail — "bones, small bones" and moves on. Appropriate given warnings.
- Wells' confession: (911–933) is credible, unflinching. The "walked in the middle of the road so the dust wouldn't show" (919) is a chilling detail. His need to know if man is real (923) is human.
- Cross' moral complexity: not forgiving easily, doing duty despite friendship (935, 937). His silence toward Callie (937) feels honest.

## What didn't (cite chapter + line numbers)
- Palm/shaking motif overdone: Wells' open palm/shaking called out multiple times (880–882, 917, 931). Trust reader; we've seen this before (Ch3).
- Callie's moral navel-gazing: (927) — "Are you so different..." question is interesting but runs too long internally; might work if shorter or implied.
- Predictable reaction beats: "he had spent thirty-two years believing no one had seen" (893) — narrator explaining again.
- Repetitive closing: "she never ran" (943–944) is fine sentiment, but could be implied.

## Cracks & tics (chapter:line)
- Chapter:828 — cove imagery effective
- Chapter:919 — middle of road detail (brutally effective)
- Chapter:880–882,917,931 — palm/shaking over-repeated
- Chapter:927 — extended self-comparison
- Chapter:935 — Cross doing duty over friendship
