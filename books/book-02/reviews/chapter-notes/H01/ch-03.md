# H01 — Book 2 Ch 3: The Open Palm (lines 264–401)
**Running impression:** 2/5 · **DNF risk:** low

## Gut reaction (in Nigel's voice)
Well now. This is where the thing either stands up as a mystery or tiptoes into cozy sleuthery. The school visit is well-structured, but the moment the principal smiles and offers tea, I start checking his palms. Which is exactly what Callie does — she notices "open palm" repeatedly (298–299, 307, 310, 322–323, 342–347, 350) and I approve of that noticing. But there's a lot of noticing, then telling us what she notices. Show, don't narrate the noticing quite so much.

## What worked (cite chapter + line numbers — begrudgingly, if at all)
- Deliberate handshake: palm shown, closed later (298–299, 322–323) is a physical clue the reader can track. Effective, specific.
- Motivated research: cipher skeleton (270–283), logical step to school (284–286). The detective work feels grounded in bookselling context.
- Ambler interview pacing: the humor (316) makes the shift when Lauren's name drops (320–324) genuinely tense. That worked.
- Sheriff confrontation: his walls protecting a friend (376–379) mirrors town blindness, creates genuine conflict for Callie.
- "Deliberate" as motif (392–393) shows Callie learning her own rule.

## What didn't (cite chapter + line numbers)
- Excessive repetition of the palm motif: it’s called out too many times (298, 310, 322, 342, 346, 347, 350). Trust reader to remember; once highlighted, move on.
- Foretelling/prolepsis: "She would not be forgiven for this" (394) and "She knew what it would cost..." (396) keep pre-announcing consequences rather than letting them land.
- Costual navel-gazing: (266–269) again with "the danger of it" and the finding letting her stop thinking about the rest. We've covered this tic.
- Ambler's confession-like monologue (330–336) feels a bit staged — polished story of grief; plausible, but too neatly packaged.

## Cracks & tics (chapter:line)
- Chapter:322–350 — palm mentioned 6+ times (motif overplayed)
- Chapter:394,396 — repeated cost-foretelling
- Chapter:316 — laughter despite wariness; works but feels borderline cozy
- Chapter:376 — "sheltering a man he sat with... the sheltering looked the same as the blindness" (solid insight)
