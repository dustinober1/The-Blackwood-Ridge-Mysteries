## Gut reaction (in Wren's voice)
The ending earns itself on specifics, not on the machinery. Doris turning Lauren's photo to the wall, holding it there for ten seconds, fifteen, then turning it back — that's a human gesture, messy and unfixable, and the book trusts it. The oiled gate at the end ("the oiling was the garden club's last tenderness for the woman who had built it") is the best callback in the book. But the author's copy-paste reflex shows: the "not-wiping" beat at 8:1065 is a near-verbatim rerun of 7:921, the ice-over-water aphorism family closes with "moving water is heavier than still water," and "its own" aphorisms are now at seven. Eight chapters, eight quiet reflective endings. The final polysyndeton ("the shop was hers, and the glass was hers, and the tea was almost right, and that was enough") is warm but it's the same move the book has made every chapter, one last time.
