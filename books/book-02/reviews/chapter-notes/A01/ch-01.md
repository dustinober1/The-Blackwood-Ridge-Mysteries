# A01 — Book 2 Ch 1: The Estate Lot (lines 50–145)
**Running impression:** 4/5 · **DNF risk:** none · **AI-smell:** mild

## Gut reaction (in Wren's voice)
Cold open, and it mostly earns it. The peonies going brown at the edges because no one on the council deadheaded them is a real observation, not a stock image — that's the kind of useless, specific detail a living eye picks up. The prose leans on rhythm harder than I'd like: three-part lists everywhere, em-dashes stacked through every other paragraph, and the brass glass doing its stock-object work as the mother's-glass emotional shorthand. But the sentences don't resolve too neatly — "You could walk on it. You did not quite trust it" is the kind of asymmetry I can't fake. The ostracism is shown in gestures (Harlan's broom, Donna's slow key) instead of stated. Not slop. Not yet.
