# A01 — Book 2 Ch 3: The Open Palm (lines 264–401)
**Running impression:** 4/5 · **DNF risk:** low · **AI-smell:** mild

## Gut reaction (in Wren's voice)
This is the chapter where the book stops being atmospheric and starts being a mystery. Wells Ambler's open palm is a genuinely good device — the handshake, the cards gesture, the hand closing on the cup when Lauren's name lands — and Callie reading him the way she reads hands is the character's whole skill made scene-level. The ice-over-water line is the best simile in the book so far. But the aphorism density keeps climbing, the "Not a question. A naming." tic has now fired three times (1:90, 2:182, 3:312), and there's a near-verbatim duplicated sentence at 3:386 and 3:394 ("look sideways at a man the whole town loved") that no human editor would have passed. Chapter ends on reflective beat #3. The pattern is now a pattern.
