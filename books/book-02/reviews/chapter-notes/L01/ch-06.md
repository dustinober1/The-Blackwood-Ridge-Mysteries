## Gut reaction (in Imogen's voice)

Linda Parrish's "bright loud voice of a woman performing reasonableness" (712) is the first knife; the archive key formalized on council letterhead (714) is the second; the garden club splitting over Marguerite's legacy (716–719) is the third. Eli's arrival (744–784) is the pivot — not rescue, but a seed. His question "How do you know?" (764) and Callie's true answer (768–770) make the seeing teachable. Cross's call (790–806) is the procedural door opening — "The location detail is specific enough" (800) — and Mae's off-page intervention is the grace note the chapter earns.

## Thematic ambition (documents/truth/authorship — chapter:line)
The chapter's thesis: a town authors its own silence. Line 714: Wells's warmth as a document — "he had spoken with warmth, and the warmth had done what warmth does." Line 718–719: the garden club, built by Marguerite to hold the town together, pulling apart over "the thing she had written down in flowers" — the document fracturing the community it was meant to bind. Line 724–725: the word *loved* and the word *killed* cannot share a room — the document (the cipher) forcing a collision the town's social documents (groceries, Thursdays, kindness) had kept separate. Line 768–770: the hand as a choosing — authorship as moral act. Line 800: Cross's "The location detail is specific enough" — the document crossing the threshold from suspicion to evidence.
## Formal coherence (structure, chapter:line)
Four days, four escalations: Monday (Linda), Tuesday (archive key), Wednesday (garden club, Doris), Thursday (Harlan, Eli, Cross). The structure is the town's closing ranks made temporal. The middle section (730–741) is a deliberate pause — the protagonist at her lowest, the prose allowed to breathe in the silence. Eli's scene (744–784) breaks the isolation not with plot but with craft transmission — the seeing becomes a lineage. Cross's call (790–806) arrives exactly when the chapter has earned it: the procedural consequence of Chapter 5's "I'll think about it." The chapter ends on Callie not sleeping (812) — the body trusting the morning, the glass warm in her hand.
