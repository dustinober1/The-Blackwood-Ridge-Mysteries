## What the revisions changed for me

Nothing I loved is gone. The tea is still almost right (8:1080–1084), "She never ran" still lands, Mae still breaks my heart. What's different is the book trusts me more: the ending of chapter 2 now stops on "A girl who had not come home" and lets it sit instead of explaining the cost, and I felt the weight *more*, not less. The new beat where the lake proves what the archive couldn't (7:862) closed a small door I hadn't realized was left open. And Callie rides out to the cove with Cross now (7:824) — which is somehow even more him: he doesn't hand her the keys, he puts her in the passenger seat of his own cruiser. That's the bounded partnership exactly.

## New issues introduced?

None that I found. The flowers are the same flowers; ch8's bed now reads "Betrayal" the way it did in ch2 (8:1008), which I only mention because I went back to check and it matches.

## Verdict (one line, in Dottie's voice)

Same five stars, softer heart — they edited the polish off and the love stayed put.
