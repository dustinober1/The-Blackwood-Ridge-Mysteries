## What the revisions changed for me

My flip condition was: "cut every sentence that names a posture the prose has already staged… If the prose trusted its own evidence, this would be a four-star novel." That is, nearly verbatim, what was done. The book now ends on image and declaration rather than analysis — the final tea paragraph states the life instead of auditing it — and the difference is exactly the difference between a book that explains its argument and a book that wins it. What remains of the old tic (one verdict line at the arrest, the four-word ch5 close) is now legible as deliberate structure rather than habit, because it is no longer everywhere.

The must-fixes touch my concerns too: the ch7 meta-leak ("as the bible had said") is replaced with the book's own earlier phrasing (7:828), which restores the fictional contract; the flower cipher's one inconsistency (Betrayal/Infidelity) is harmonized (8:1008), which matters for a book whose entire method is semantic precision.

## New issues introduced?

The new closure beat (7:862) and the reworked transit (7:824, 7:868) are in key with the book — plain declaratives, no new gloss. The ch8 ending keeps "and that was enough," which is the thesis; ending on it once, without the doubled hammer, is the right amount.

## Verdict (one line, in Imogen's voice)

The prose now trusts its own hand — the four stars I said were hiding in it.
