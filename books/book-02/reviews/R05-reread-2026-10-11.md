## What the revisions changed for me

My flip condition was "cut the three tumbler metaphors and the bible line and it's a clean 5★." Both done, plus a haircut I didn't ask for that I now want in every book I read this year: the chapters don't all end on the same sigh anymore. Chapter 4 ends mid-motion ("She turned to the next plate"), chapter 6 ends on Eli's question carrying into the dive, chapter 7 ends with her holding the glass in the quiet — no verdict stamped on it. As a binge reader I feel the difference in the thumb: every chapter break used to be a period; now some of them are commas, and I read two extra chapters before bed because of it.

The archive-closure beat (7:862) is three sentences and fixes the one plot thread I'd have complained about on a forum: the lake proves it, the locked archive stops mattering, move on.
