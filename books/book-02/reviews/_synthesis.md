## Consensus issues (3+ personas)

1. **Prose cadence / quiet-reflective endings** (A01, H01, L01, R05 — 4 personas): every chapter ends on a reflective beat (M02 logged the pattern at 1:136, 2:258, 3:397, 4:552, 5:700, 6:812, 7:947); tricolon/aphorism machinery (5:566); summary intrusion naming what the scene already staged (L01); repeated decode metaphors "the tumbler turned" (R05: 3:389, 4:412, 5:562). Most-read weakness in the panel.
2. **Off-screen resolution / convenience** (M01, I02, L01 — 3 personas): archive key recalled via council "security audit" (M01: 4:442–456); Mae's intervention with Cross happens off-page (M01: 6:800–802; I02); Harriet Vailes as convenience messenger (L01: 4:438–462).
3. **The town's complicity is the real mystery** (R01, R05, M01, L01, I02 — 5 personas, consensus strength): warmth-as-weapon — archive closed, garden club split, Harlan stops coming in (6:712–728) — praised across personas.
4. **Mae's late confession and Eli's apprenticeship** (R01, R05, M02, M01, I02 — 5 personas, consensus strength): "I did the thing I always do" (5:622); "could you teach me" (6:776).
5. **Cozy contract honored / restraint** (R01, M01, I02, H01 — 4 personas, consensus strength): recovery of remains is a sentence, not a scene (7:866); confession spoken, not depicted (7:915–919).

## Polarizing issues (love/hate splits)

1. **Prose texture (5 vs 2–3)**: the same cadence reads "plain metaphors you feel in your bones" (R01) and "genuine observation" (A01) to the 5★ cluster, but "machine's polish" (A01), "poetic tics" (H01), and "summary intrusion" (L01) to the low scorers. The central polarizer.
2. **Mystery fairness (5 vs 2)**: fair-play purists see a full clue ladder with zero asymmetry (M01, A01); the guess-the-killer reader sees a one-suspect pool and the answer at Ch 3:290 — "not a puzzle, a label" (M02). Same structure, opposite verdicts.
3. **Pacing (5 vs 2–3)**: "slow burn, deliberate, every chapter earns its length" (R01) vs "too much circling/cost foretelling" (H01) and 500–600 lines of confirmation (M02).
4. **Character & arc (5 vs 2)**: "specific, changing" (R01, I02) vs "competent but over-narrated" (H01).

## Headline for the author

The book's strengths (town-as-antagonist, Mae, Eli, restraint) are consensus; its weakness is a repeated quiet-aphorism cadence and a one-suspect mystery that satisfies fair-play readers but leaves guess-the-killer readers done by Chapter 3. If Book 3 fixes one thing: vary the chapter-ending beats and widen the suspect pool.
