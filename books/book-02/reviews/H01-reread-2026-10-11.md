## What the revisions changed for me

My flip condition was: "Cut the cost-foretelling and the not-peace loops by ~30%… trust the facts and silences to carry weight. That alone would flip to 3★." That is what happened, and I'll be damned if the book isn't better for it in ways beyond my request — the fresh closure of the archive thread (7:862) is three flat sentences doing the work a paragraph of circling used to do, and the cove chapter now opens with a man driving in silence instead of a woman narrating her drive. The puzzle was already the honest kind; now the telling of it doesn't keep elbowing me in the ribs.

## New issues introduced?

The confession's wet-eyes beat (7:114-equivalent) keeps its first, fullest statement and the Mae echo at the end now varies it — I checked, because that's the sort of echo that doubles back. It varies. Fine.

## Verdict (one line, in Nigel's voice)

Three stars, grudgingly promoted to genuinely — the book stopped telling me the weight and let the water have it.
