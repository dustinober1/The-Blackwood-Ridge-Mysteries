## Structural integrity check after the pass

- **The spine holds.** Cipher → wet hemline → map → body → confession; the ch3 midpoint; the ch6 dark night — all intact. The ch4 ending change ("She turned to the next plate. Marguerite had left her more," 4:552) actually *sharpens* the act turn: the chapter now exits on forward motion instead of a resolved sigh.

- **The new transit is better blocking, not just canon compliance.** Riding out together gives the pre-dive chapter a held-breath opening ("Neither of them talked," 7:824) and "Cross was out of the cruiser first" (7:830) is free characterization the old parking beat didn't earn. - **Nothing load-bearing was cut.** I went looking for over-trim in the families: every surviving instance is a first-use or a payoff; every cut instance has a survivor within reach. This is what a disciplined density pass looks like — the voice is untouched, the machinery is thinned.
