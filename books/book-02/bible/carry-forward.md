# Carry-Forward into Book 2

> tight-mode summary of where the recurring characters physically and
> emotionally stand at the start of Book 2, what is resolved, and what is open.
> The per-book `/novella-concept` and `/novella-draft-chapter` skills must
> honor this. Source of truth: the published state of Book 1.

## Timeline position

Book 2 opens after the events of Book 1 ("The Annotated Murder"): Gus Ralston
arrested (Day 13), the scandal broken, the town fractured. In the days/weeks
following, Callie reclaims the shop, the tea, the glass — and finds the
botanical book with an unknown hand's marginalia. That discovery is the hook
into Book 2.

## The town (setting state)

- Wounded. Knows the truth about its founding fraud (Silas Ralston's land
  theft and the murdered Reverend Hartwell).
- Some grateful, some resentful. The old-money hierarchy is cracked.
- **Gus Ralston imprisoned.** The Ralston name disgraced. The Historical
  Society closed. The town's power structure permanently altered.
- Callie is now "the woman who pulled the thread" — reputation shifted from
  quiet shopkeeper to the person who sees what the town spent generations
  hiding. In Book 2 she is a **pariah**: the town doesn't want her involved.

## Recurring characters — physical & emotional start state

### Callie Thorne (POV)
- **Physical:** Still owns The Foxed Page; lives in the apartment upstairs.
  Has reclaimed the shop, the bergamot tea, the magnifying glass. Holds the
  botanical book — the new mystery.
- **Emotional:** Reclaimed her life from grief's hold, but the reclamation is
  fresh and untested under new pressure. Relationships strained but intact.
  No longer the invisible shopkeeper; not yet accepted as anything else.
  Carries the cost of having wounded the town she lives in.
- **Open thread:** The botanical book's unknown marginalia.

### Mae Hartwell
- **Physical:** In her home (Hartwell House); the family Bible with its
  damning margin note is still on the parlor shelf.
- **Emotional:** Confessed her knowledge of the family secret in Book 1.
  Relationship with Callie strained but not broken. She brought soup after
  the arrest — the door is open, but different. She knocks now where she used
  to walk in.
- **Open thread:** The fracture. Whether the surrogate-family bond heals,
  hardens, or quietly calcifies. Mae's promise to Eleanor ("look after
  Callie") still binds her even through the wound.

### Sheriff Dalton Cross
- **Physical:** At the Sheriff's Office; department unchanged in resources.
- **Emotional:** Shook Callie's hand at the end of Book 1 — grudging respect
  established, first gesture of professional equality. Will be less
  dismissive of her in future — but **still procedural and resistant** (per
  Book 2 slot). The respect is real; it is not yet partnership.
- **Open thread:** How he handles a case where Callie's methods are again
  required. The handshak did not make him an ally.

### Eli Townsend
- **Physical:** Still at the Ridgeway Auction House; still in Blackwood Ridge.
- **Emotional:** Cleared. Standing taller — has started introducing himself to
  customers instead of hiding in the back. Callie earned his trust; he
  doesn't know how to thank her. No longer acting guilty out of anxiety.
- **Open thread:** Book 2 plants the **apprenticeship seed** — Eli begins
  asking Callie questions about archival work. He is background in this book,
  not a subplot, but the curiosity is the origin of his later arc.

## Resolved vs. open

**Resolved by Book 1:**
- Pike's murder solved; Gus Ralston imprisoned.
- Eli cleared of suspicion.
- The founding fraud exposed (Silas Ralston's theft; Reverend Hartwell's
  murder).
- Callie's grief-grip loosened; she reclaimed the shop, tea, and glass.

**Open into Book 2:**
- The botanical book's unknown hand — who wrote the marginalia, and what it
  confesses (the Book 2 engine).
- The Callie–Mae fracture (does it heal or harden?).
- Callie's standing in town (pariah; useful, not welcome).
- Eli's trajectory toward archival work (seed, not yet sprout).
- Whether bergamot tea "tastes right yet" under renewed pressure.

## Constraints for Book 2

- Do **not** re-expose the founding fraud — that case is closed and public.
- Do **not** rehabilitate Callie's town reputation in this book — the slot
  requires she remain a pariah who *solves* the case but *gains no goodwill*.
- The botanical book must be the same object planted at the end of Book 1;
  treat its provenance as established canon.
- Eli's role stays background; his apprenticeship is a seed, not a storyline.
