## Book 2 obligations (from series-outline.md)

- **The botanical book pays off.** The hook planted at the end of Book 1 (the
  book with an unknown hand's marginalia, found among Callie's reclaimed
  stock) is the engine of this case.
- **The decoding-moment structure deepens.** The franchise signature — slowing
  to a crawl over the shapes of letters, then snapping short when meaning
  clicks — should develop, not just repeat.
- **The town-as-chorus shows post-Book 1 wariness.** Snapshot glimpses of
  townspeople should register that Callie is now feared/resented, not just
  known.
- **Bergamot tea returns as Callie's compass** — does it taste right yet?
  (Book 1 ended with Callie reclaiming "the tea"; this book tests whether the
  reclamation holds under new pressure.)

## Motifs and callbacks (series canon — preserve)

- **Eleanor's brass magnifying glass:** Callie's inherited tool. In Book 1's
  climax she set it down (loosening Eleanor's grip) and picked it up again,
  now meaning something different. Entering Book 2 it is *Callie's chosen
  tool, not Eleanor's ghost* — but Eleanor intrusions remain in play.
- **Margin notes as voices:** Handwriting carries the living presence of the
  writer. Book 2's botanical annotations are a NEW hand — establish who this
  hand is, what it feels like to read. Callie reads hands the way she reads
  faces (canon from Book 1 Ch1).
- **Bergamot tea:** Eleanor's tea, Pike's tea, Callie's daily ritual of
  remembrance. Use as emotional weather gauge across this book.
- **Rain on windows:** Safety / inside-while-world-stays-outside. Inverts
  under threat (rain on archive windows while Callie works alone, no longer
  safe).
- **The Foxed Page back room:** Eleanor's workspace, where Eleanor died,
  Callie's fortress. Carries triple weight (sanctuary/tomb/workspace).
- **The decoding moment (signature move):** Slow to a crawl over letter
  shapes, pen pressure, the ghost of the hand; then meaning clicks and the
  sentence snaps short.
- **The two-register pivot:** A warm scene undercut by a dread detail; the
  prose changes temperature without announcing it.
- **Eleanor intrusions:** Unprompted sensory memories of Eleanor revealing how
  close to the surface Callie's grief still is.
- **The town as chorus:** Brief, specific snapshots of townspeople
  establishing the community Callie is risking.

## Emotional scars entering Book 2

- **Callie:** Unprocessed grief for Eleanor — transmuted into workaholism and
  isolation — is *loosened* but not resolved by Book 1. New pressure will test
  whether the reclamation holds. Trigger: finding someone alone in a room
  (flashes to Eleanor).
- **Callie:** The guilt of having liked Pike but never naming it friendship
  until after he was dead — the pattern of appreciating relationships only in
  retrospect. Watch for repetition with new characters.
- **Mae:** Decades of complicit silence, now broken. The wound from Book 1 is
  fresh. She still fills rooms with warmth so no one asks what she's hiding.

## Promises and debts in play

- **Mae's promise to Eleanor** ("Look after Callie"): kept, but now in tension
  with the fracture from Book 1. Mae still brings soup — but she knocks now,
  where she used to walk in.
- **Eli's debt to his father** ("Call when you've figured things out"):
  unpaid. Accrues in the background. Book 2 plants the seed of Eli asking
  Callie about archival work (the apprenticeship origin).

## Relationship residue entering Book 2

- **Callie & Mae:** The deepest wound. Understanding doesn't heal. Mae still
  brings soup, but she knocks. The door is open but different.
- **Callie & Cross:** Grudging respect replaces antagonism. Cross shook her
  hand at the end of Book 1 — first gesture of professional equality. He will
  be less dismissive, but still procedural/resistant (per Book 2 slot).
- **Callie & Eli:** She saved him; he doesn't know how to thank her. He's
  standing taller — introducing himself to customers instead of hiding. Trust
  earned. Potential ally. Book 2: Eli begins asking Callie questions about
  archival work (apprenticeship seed).

## Details to carry forward

- **The botanical book** — found at end of Book 1 among Callie's reclaimed
  stock, carrying an unknown hand's marginalia. This is Book 2's keystone
  document. Establish its physical specifics early.
- **Callie's ink stains** — she stopped trying to scrub them by Book 1's
  midpoint (small acceptance shift). Maintain.
- **Gus's hands tell** (canon — Callie reads hands like handwriting; over-
  control is the tell, not tremor). Applies to how Callie reads the new
  cast: watch hands.
- **The signature log in the archives** — who signed in and when becomes
  evidence. Maintain as a living mechanic.
