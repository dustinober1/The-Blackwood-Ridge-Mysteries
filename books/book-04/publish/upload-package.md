## Required final platform checks

1. Upload the EPUB and separate cover image.
2. Open the retailer previewer or Kindle Previewer.
3. Inspect the cover, title/copyright material, contents/navigation, all eight chapter starts, scene breaks, italics, ending, and back matter.
4. Confirm the cover is legible at thumbnail size and is not cropped.
5. Confirm the description, keywords, categories, price, territories, DRM choice, and exclusivity choice.
6. Answer the platform's content-disclosure questions from the actual production history.
7. Submit for publication.

Do not mark `publish: complete` in the repository until the retailer has accepted the book and its detail page is live.
