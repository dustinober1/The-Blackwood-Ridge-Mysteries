# Packaging — The Archive Fire

## Package role

This file is the Book 4 package-guidance companion to the draft retailer-listing direction at `../publish/listing.md`. It prepares cover/package decisions and series-continuity checks only. It does **not** publish, upload, or generate a final retail file.

## Positioning for this novella

### Subcategory and also-bought neighborhood

**Primary path:** Kindle eBooks > Mystery, Thriller & Suspense > Cozy Mysteries  
**Secondary paths:** Mystery > Amateur Sleuth, Mystery > Women Sleuths, bookish / archival mystery readers

Book 4 remains in the atmospheric archival cozy lane. Its book-specific identity is archive damage, salvage/reconstruction, an old sheriff ledger, and the formal partnership turn between Callie and Cross.

### Differentiators

1. **Archive-as-victim visual hook** — the town's memory is damaged, not just a person.
2. **Reconstruction mystery device** — Callie reads what remains: water lines, duplicate cards, ash-gray ledgers, and record habits.
3. **Cross partnership turn** — the sheriff formally asks Callie to help and then follows the reconstructed record into his own office's history.
4. **Series momentum** — Callie advances from grudging utility to reluctant consultant without becoming a police officer or action sleuth.

## Cover guidance

### Series-template inheritance

> **Locked from Books 1-3:** elegant gold serif title treatment, deep-plum / charcoal atmospheric palette, warm parchment or aged-ivory paper tones, consistent series-label placement, consistent author-name placement, and the brass magnifying glass as Callie's recurring visual anchor.

For Book 4, rotate the focal object to a smoke-stained ledger / damaged archive table. The design should still read instantly as a sibling to *The Annotated Murder*, *The Botanical Confession*, and *The Challenger*.

### Visual concepts

**Concept A: The Smoke-Stained Ledger (recommended)**  
An old ledger on a wooden salvage table under warm lamplight, its page edges darkened and warped, with careful paper labels, an archive-green folder, a brass magnifying glass, and a small brass key charm nearby. The image should feel quiet, bookish, and investigative rather than disaster-forward.

**Concept B: The Salvage Table**  
A temporary archive sorting table in The Foxed Page back room: labeled folders, towels, aged ivory papers, a brass magnifying glass, and a single damaged index card with unreadable marks. Warm lamplight against deep plum shadow.

**Concept C: The Night Ledger**  
Close view of a dark clothbound night ledger with a water line across the cover, an old sheriff-office label, and the magnifying glass catching a thin gold reflection. Strong clue-object image, subtle and elegant.

### Color palette

**Aged Jewel Tones — Ash Variant**

- Deep plum / shadow: `#5B2C6F`
- Tarnished gold / brass / title: `#B8860B`
- Aged ivory / paper: `#FFFFF0`
- Archive green: `#4B6043`
- Ash gray: `#696969`
- Smoke brown: `#6B4F3A`
- Soft ember accent: `#A65A3A` used sparingly

### Typography

- **Title font:** Same elegant serif family as Books 1-3.
- **Author name:** Same size, color, and placement as previous series covers.
- **Series label:** `The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries · Book 4` in the same position and approximate size as Books 1-3.
- **Hierarchy:** Series label smallest, title largest, author name second.
- **Thumbnail rule:** Title must remain legible at 150 px width; damaged-ledger details should read as texture, not clutter.

### Composition

- **Upper third:** Title and series label in locked position.
- **Middle third:** Smoke-stained ledger, salvage table, key charm, and brass magnifying glass.
- **Lower third:** Author name and optional folder/tag detail.
- **Negative space:** Preserve breathing room. Avoid filling the cover with debris.
- **Series motif:** The brass magnifying glass must appear.

### Avoid

- No flames, body, panic, or disaster-action imagery.
- No police badge / procedural cover language.
- No horror, thriller, or true-crime visual signals.
- No cheerful pastel cozy style.
- No generated text in the artwork itself.
