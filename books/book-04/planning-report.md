## Core premise

After the Town Archives are damaged and retired clerk Ruth Mallory is found dead, Sheriff Cross formally asks Callie to help reconstruct what was lost. The damaged record points to Cross's predecessor, Roland Tavis, and a 1991 Lila Crowe case that a present-day civic leader still needs to keep smoothed over.

## Mystery summary

- **Victim:** Ruth Mallory.
- **Surface explanation:** tragic archive incident.
- **Hidden present case:** Ruth was targeted because of what she found.
- **Historical record:** Lila Crowe, 1991; old sheriff-office record failure involving Roland Tavis and Benedict Calder.
- **Culprit:** Benedict Calder.
- **Resolution:** Cross acts on the reconstructed record and corrects the old departmental file. The archive remains damaged but not erased.

## Chapter outline summary

1. **Smoke Under Town Hall** — Ruth calls Callie; the archives are damaged; Cross asks Callie for help.
2. **The Salvage Table** — temporary sorting at The Foxed Page; Ruth's pocket card surfaces.
3. **A Shelf That Lied Twice** — Callie and Cross reconstruct Ruth's last pulls.
4. **The Predecessor's Hand** — midpoint; Tavis and Lila Crowe enter the case; Cross commits to reading all of it.
5. **Water Lines** — Ben Calder becomes more central; Mae remembers the old town atmosphere.
6. **Bad Procedure** — Tavis's old rationale surfaces; false paths begin clearing.
7. **The Ash Index** — Ruth's duplicate system reconstructs the proof path.
8. **The Box Asked For** — Ben is exposed; Cross corrects the old record; partnership begins.
