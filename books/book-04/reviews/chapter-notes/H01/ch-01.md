**Running impression:** 3/5 · **DNF risk:** low
## Gut reaction (in Nigel's voice)
Opens with frost on bricks and a bookseller watching the town avoid her door — writerly scene-setting that announces itself. Ruth Mallory's call at line 93 is the first living thing: dry, clipped, a clerk who knows shelves shouldn't lie. The fire arrives on schedule (line 209) and Ruth dies off-page, which is honest. Cross removes his hat outdoors (line 317) — a procedural tell that works. But Eleanor's ghost is already haunting the back room (lines 73–78, 355–356) and the "familiar narrowing" internal monologue (line 163) tells instead of shows.
