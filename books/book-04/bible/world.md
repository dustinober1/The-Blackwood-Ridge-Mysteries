# World — The Archive Fire

Book 4 remains in contemporary Blackwood Ridge, Virginia, immediately after Book 3.

## Core setting promise

The setting emphasis is archival, civic, and intimate: Town Hall, The Foxed Page, the Sheriff's Office, Hartwell House, the Old Courthouse Records Annex, and the town square under early-November weather.

## New setting pressure

The Town Archives are damaged and partially closed. The emotional focus is not spectacle; it is what happens to Callie, Cross, Eli, Mae, and the town when the place that kept the public record can no longer be treated as stable.

## Atmosphere

Cold stone, wet paper, tarnished brass, archive green folders, ash-gray mornings, lamplight, first frost, and the brittle quiet before holiday warmth.

## Location rules

- The Foxed Page back room becomes a shared sorting space, challenging Callie's instinct to work alone.
- The Sheriff's Office carries Cross's present duty and his predecessor's old shadow.
- Hartwell House remains a thaw location where practical help matters more than apology.
- The Old Courthouse Records Annex provides secondary records and bureaucratic texture.
- The town square remains the chorus: watching, uneasy, useful before it is welcoming.

## Avoid

- No action set pieces.
- No supernatural explanations.
- No broad conspiracy.
- No full-town corruption frame.
- No courthouse-procedural takeover; Callie's record-reading remains the center.
