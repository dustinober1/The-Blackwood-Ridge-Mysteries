# Timeline — The Archive Fire

## Deep backstory surfaced in Book 4

- **1991, late autumn:** Nineteen-year-old Lila Crowe dies after a Ridge Road hit-and-run following a harvest dance. The case is filed as unresolved and quietly allowed to cool.
- **1991, same week:** Sheriff Roland Tavis receives information that points toward Benedict Calder, then a teenager from a politically connected family. The information is not entered cleanly into the official case file.
- **1991-2026:** The public story becomes ordinary tragedy: a bad road, a dark night, a young woman gone. The private record survives in night-ledger fragments, carbon slips, and staff memory.
- **Years before Book 4:** Ruth Mallory works as county clerk and maintains duplicate habits for fragile / volatile records. She knows Eleanor Thorne professionally and respects her archival rigor.

## Immediate series lead-in

- **Book 3 final days:** Harlan Beech is arrested for the Vivian Larter case. Folios 43-44 and the Beech family letter become recoverable/actionable. Cross tells Callie her reading was useful, but not yet a partnership.
- **Days later:** The town is still absorbing the Historical Society scandal when the Town Archives become the next wounded institution.

## Book 4 present timeline

### Day 1 — Archive fire

- Ruth Mallory calls Callie about "a shelf that lied twice" and an old night ledger.
- Fire damages the Town Archives under Town Hall.
- Ruth is found dead in the aftermath.
- Cross asks Callie to help read what burned.

### Day 2 — Salvage triage

- The Foxed Page back room becomes a temporary salvage table.
- Mae brings practical supplies; Eli assists with damaged records.
- Ruth's charred pocket card reveals `TAVIS / CROWE / B.C. / night book`.

### Day 3 — Damage map

- Callie and Cross reconstruct Ruth's final record pulls.
- The relevant damaged bay held old sheriff night-ledger materials.
- Simon Vale and Clara Hensley become plausible false paths.

### Day 4 — Midpoint: predecessor record

- Ruth's death is treated as a present-day case, not a simple fire tragedy.
- Lila Crowe's 1991 record emerges.
- Cross sees Roland Tavis's old procedural hand in the damaged record.
- Cross formally asks Callie to continue.

### Day 5 — Water lines

- Salvage work deepens.
- Ben Calder offers public help.
- Mae remembers the 1991 harvest dance and the Calder truck rumors.
- Callie identifies the fake note as unlike Ruth's real record habits.

### Day 6 — Bad procedure

- Cross visits Roland Tavis.
- Tavis reveals the old thinking: keep the town whole, protect the powerful, smooth the record.
- False suspects begin clearing.
- Eli notices the duplicate sequence matters.

### Day 7 — Ash index

- Ruth's duplicate protocol yields a reconstructable path.
- Ben Calder reveals guilty knowledge.
- Callie matches the fake note's smoothing language to Ben's committee language.
- Cross has enough to proceed.

### Day 8 — Resolution

- Cross and Callie confront Ben at the temporary archive room.
- Ben is exposed as the Book 4 culprit.
- Cross files a supplemental record naming Tavis's old failure.
- Archive restoration begins; not all is saved, but the record is no longer smooth around the lie.

## Ending state after Book 4

- **Callie:** Reluctant consultant; more able to share the work.
- **Cross:** Formal working partner; willing to correct his office's old record.
- **Eli:** Reconstruction skill canonized.
- **Mae:** Thaw advanced through shared work.
- **Town:** Still uneasy, but now brings records to the table instead of only withholding them.
- **Archive:** Damaged but not gone.
