# Carry-Forward into Book 4

> Tight-mode continuity summary for the start of Book 4. Future drafting must honor this file and the series-level bible.

## Timeline position

Book 4 opens in early November 2026, days after the end of *The Challenger*. Harlan Beech has been arrested for the Vivian Larter case. Larter's stylometric work stands. The Beech amendment and recovered folios make Callie's original Wren reading incomplete but not false. Cross has seen Callie's reading become actionable in the room where it happened, but he explicitly said it was not a partnership yet.

## Ending state after Book 3

### Callie Thorne

- **Status:** Not welcome, not yet — but changed. The town recognizes her as useful rather than merely dangerous.
- **Reputation:** Shifted from pariah to grudging utility. Linda Parrish's held wave is the public temperature: not warmth, but no longer total avoidance.
- **Method:** The glass is hers and has survived being tested by Larter's instrument. Callie now knows her reading eye has blind spots and must sometimes work beside other methods.
- **Emotional state:** The forbidden question has a provisional answer: she is different from a keeper who buries because she brings the box into the light. Book 4 must test whether she can do that when the archive itself is damaged.
- **Open wound:** Eleanor trigger remains active: women alone among records, found too late, strike directly at Callie's grief.

### Sheriff Dalton Cross

- **Status:** Cross has begun to trust Callie's reading, but he has not formalized partnership.
- **Book 3 exit:** He saw Beech's hand and said, "For the file, your reading was useful." He also said it did not make the arrangement a partnership.
- **Book 4 slot:** The archive fire forces him to ask directly. Book 4 earns the formal-consultant turn.
- **Pressure:** His predecessor's old record wounds the institution he represents. His arc is not about defending the old office; it is about filing what should have been filed.
- **Tells:** Neck rub = out of depth; jaw set = friendship/duty or institutional-duty stress; hat removal = respect/warming.

### Eli Townsend

- **Status:** Saturday apprentice at The Foxed Page; increasingly steady; helpful with records.
- **Book 3 exit:** He helped by making copies and staying out of the final confrontation. He did not become the answer.
- **Book 4 role:** Learns archival reconstruction: duplicate indexing, water-damaged records, carbon impressions, salvage boundaries, and chain-of-custody caution.
- **Constraint:** Do not surface the long-arc manipulation thread. Do not make Eli over-solve. His skill growth should read as apprenticeship.
- **Open thread:** Father's debt remains unpaid.

### Mae Hartwell

- **Status:** Slow thaw with Callie continues. Mae knocks now where she once walked in.
- **Book 3 exit:** Kitchen thaw deepened around keeping vs hiding. Mae named that Callie is a keeper too.
- **Book 4 role:** Helps with the salvage table. Her offering is work rather than soup — a form Callie can accept.
- **Pressure:** Mae remembers Lila Crowe and the town's old silence around the Calder name. She gives context, not a solution.

### The town

- **Status:** Tired of buried truths and still unable to look away.
- **Current temperature:** Not fully hostile, not welcoming. Callie's usefulness is becoming public but uncomfortable.
- **Book 4 pressure:** The Town Archives burn immediately after the Historical Society scandal, making the whole town feel that its records are unstable.

## Prior cases — do not retcon

- **Book 1:** Wendell Pike's case exposed the Ralston/Hartwell/Wren founding scandal. Gus Ralston is no longer a free public power. The land fraud and Reverend Hartwell death are public truth.
- **Book 2:** Lauren Coalter's 1994 disappearance was solved. Wells Ambler confessed. Marguerite Dabney's botanical cipher was vindicated.
- **Book 3:** Vivian Larter's case was solved. Document X / folios 43-44 and the family letter were recovered/actionable. Larter's method stands. Callie was incomplete, not fraudulent.

## Open threads into Book 4

- **Document Y:** A second missing record inferred in Book 3 remains unresolved. Do not name Eli. Do not make it the Book 4 central solution. It may appear only as a quiet archival seam.
- **Cross partnership:** Book 4 must formalize this without turning Callie into an employee or deputy.
- **Archive vulnerability:** Town Archives are Callie's dual home. Their damage should echo Eleanor without repeating Book 1.
- **Eli's competence:** Grows through reconstruction skill.
- **Mae's thaw:** Continues by shared work, not full emotional reset.
