# New Cast — The Archive Fire

## Ruth Mallory

Retired county clerk and Book 4 victim. Precise, prickly, ethically stubborn. Her duplicate habits and indexing style create the fair-play path through the damaged archive.

## Benedict "Ben" Calder

Councilman and archive-redevelopment chair. Publicly helpful, privately dependent on a clean public story. His smoothing civic language is the key to reading him.

## Roland Tavis

Former sheriff and Cross's predecessor. He represents old procedure used to protect comfort rather than truth. He is morally central to the old Lila Crowe record but should not be the present-day culprit.

## Lila Crowe

The 1991 victim whose corrected record matters. Keep her human through ordinary details: harvest dance, library cart, a scarf, her name in an old ledger. Do not reduce her to a plot object.

## Simon Vale

Restoration contractor. Practical, defensive, proud of his work. A red herring because he argued with Ruth and looks careless around the damaged archive. His actual flaw is ego and sloppy documentation, not Ruth's death.

## Clara Hensley

Assistant archivist. Young, underpaid, anxious about being blamed for every missing thing. She lies about a key-log entry because she removed a minor box without permission for personal reasons. Red herring.

## Nell Pritchard

Historical Society volunteer and donor organizer. Socially polished, embarrassed by donation-record smoothing, and terrified that every small irregularity now looks like a town-shattering secret. Red herring.

## Deputy Bell

Returning support from Book 3. Photographs damaged records and helps Cross make Callie's reading portable without turning the series into a forensic lane.

## Use guidance

- Keep the cast intimate.
- Every new character should connect to archive damage, record-keeping, or the old Lila Crowe file.
- Do not add extra suspects unless a drafting pass requires one for pacing.
- Avoid melodramatic villain behavior. Blackwood Ridge secrets are hidden through manners, procedure, and helpfulness.
