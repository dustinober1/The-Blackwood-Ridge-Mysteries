# Roland Tavis

Retired sheriff and Cross's predecessor. Tavis is an institutional mirror for Cross: a man who believes he protected Blackwood Ridge by smoothing a difficult record.

## Use

- Represents the old office Cross inherited.
- Gives the 1991 Lila Crowe material emotional and procedural weight.
- Should remain calm and paternal, not theatrical.
- Is morally central to the old record but not the Book 4 present-day antagonist.

## Voice

Short, practical, old-lawman cadence. Uses phrases like "the town couldn't take it" and "we had to be careful."

## Ending posture

The official record changes even if Tavis does not.
