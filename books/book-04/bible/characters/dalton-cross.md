# Sheriff Dalton Cross — Book 4

## State entering

Cross exits Book 3 having seen Callie's reading matter in a way he can witness. He called it useful but refused to call it a partnership. Book 4 removes that safety. The archive fire creates a records-driven case he cannot solve cleanly without Callie's help.

## External desire

Solve Ruth Mallory's case, protect the damaged record, and keep the investigation from collapsing under old departmental shame.

## Internal need

To understand that procedure is not loyalty to the office's comfort. Procedure is loyalty to the record, even when the record shows the office failed.

## Pressure points

- Ruth's final records point toward Roland Tavis, Cross's predecessor and early mentor.
- Cross's father taught him that bad procedure ruins lives; Tavis's old conduct shows how true that warning was.
- Asking Callie formally means admitting her method belongs in the room.
- The town will prefer a tidy accident story because everyone is tired of public wounds.

## Arc

Cross begins by needing Callie and resenting how visible that need is. The more the case points toward Tavis, the more he must decide whether the institution is worth protecting from its own record. He ends by filing the supplemental record and making Callie's consultation explicit. The partnership is not sentimental; it is procedural respect turned into action.

## Relationship movement

- **Callie:** Moves from "useful for the file" to formal consultant. He still uses clipped language, but he gives her access, context, and trust.
- **Eli:** Treats him as a young assistant who needs boundaries. Cross should notice Eli's competence without leaning on him as investigator.
- **Mae:** Mae can still get through to him, but Book 4 requires Cross to move without needing Mae's one-word push.
- **Roland Tavis:** Predecessor mirror. Tavis represents procedure used as burial.

## Tells to track

- Neck rub when out of depth.
- Jaw set when institutional duty conflicts with personal loyalty.
- Hat removal as respect / warming signal.
- Shorter sentences under pressure.

## Ending target

Cross arrests the Book 4 culprit, names Tavis's old failure in the official record, and acknowledges Callie's role as a working partner without making her a deputy or employee.
