# Lila Crowe

## Function

Lila Crowe is the 1991 young woman whose corrected record matters in Book 4. She should feel like a person, not a clue object.

## Human details

- Nineteen in 1991.
- Worked part-time around books / local records or the library cart.
- Went to the harvest dance before the Ridge Road incident.
- Remembered by a few older townspeople in fragments: a scarf, a laugh in the school gym, a careful signature on library slips.

## Use guidance

- Keep her ordinary and specific.
- Do not over-sentimentalize her.
- Her story should not duplicate Lauren Coalter's Book 2 disappearance. Lila's case is a public death with a smoothed record, not a missing-girl mystery.
- Mae can provide memory; Ruth's records provide structure; Cross provides the corrected official action.

## Emotional role

Lila is the person the old record failed. Book 4's resolution gives her record agency again, even though it cannot give her life back.
