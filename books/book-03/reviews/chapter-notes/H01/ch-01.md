## Gut reaction (in Nigel's voice)
A strong cold open: stranger with a portfolio and a statistical model walks into the bookshop and tells the protagonist her famous reading missed a second hand. The stylometry hook is fresh. But the bergamot tea gets its first "almost right, not quite" at line 53, Eleanor's ghost whispers at 121, and the forbidden question arrives at 125 — all three tics announced in chapter one. The town's "cruel silence" (47–49) is overwritten; Linda Parrish's averted face does the work without the narration explaining it.
## What worked (cite chapter + line numbers — begrudgingly, if at all)
- Larter's entrance (55–63): rehearsed walk, portfolio like a card laid down, accent flattened on purpose — efficient character sketch.
- The overlay images (77–81): blue for primary hand, red for secondary — gives the reader a forensic object to hold.

- Callie's restraint (83–89): doesn't defend, reads the data, lets the evidence land.
