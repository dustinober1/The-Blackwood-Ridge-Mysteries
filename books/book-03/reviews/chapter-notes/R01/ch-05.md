## Gut reaction (in Dottie's voice)
Oh, that shelf scene! Callie on her knees, finger in the ghost-space, feeling the wood remember what the paper forgot. "Paper had been here" (5:741) — simple, devastating, and so very fair-play. The whole reading-room atmosphere, the cold breath of after-hours, the single bookend on the desk... and that moment when Callie realizes she's looking at a *removal*, not a loss. My teacher's heart clapped when she said "the record had failed her on purpose."
