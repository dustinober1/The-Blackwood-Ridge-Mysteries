* **Access policy reading:** "Keys were issued. Alarm codes maintained. Item movements authorized and logged" (6:872–873) — the power that looks like procedure.
* **Mae's wisdom:** "I don't know that difference is something you get to announce about yourself" (6:1051) — profound, teacherly.

* Cross can't yet act (6:949–950) — Dottie wants justice served, and patience is hard.
