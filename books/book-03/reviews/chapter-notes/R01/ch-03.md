# R01 — Book 3 Ch 3: The Door Opens (lines 259–370)
**Running impression:** 5/5 · **DNF risk:** none

## Gut reaction (in Dottie's voice)
Oh, Callie with Eli! My, my, watching him grow from that stammering boy into someone useful and steady — it warmed this retired teacher's heart clear through tea-time. And those movement records! The careful counting of slips, the "returned, returned, returned" rhythm, and then the discovery of what *isn't* there. Forty-three and forty-four simply gone from the catalog, as if someone had written the record around a hole and made it look like order. Bless.

## What worked
* **Eli's apprenticeship:** "Six months ago he would have answered before she finished asking" (3:325–327); now he waits, listens, serves (3:339, 3:359, 3:587).
* **The catalog gap discovery:** "Forty-one. Forty-two. Forty-five. Forty-six." (3:351) — the missing tooth that names the keeper.
* **Callie's careful wording:** She refuses to name Beech as villain yet (3:531–535) — "Useful can be a mighty pretty curtain" (3:1007, echoing into Ch 6).
* **The "edited" admission:** "I was not simply wrong... I was edited" (3:665–669) — a devastating but honest moment.

## What didn't
* The horror of realizing her own foundational reading was built on an edited record (3:355–357, 3:660–664) — emotionally heavy, though fair for a mystery.
* Dottie wishes Mae had shown up to offer bread and comfort here.
