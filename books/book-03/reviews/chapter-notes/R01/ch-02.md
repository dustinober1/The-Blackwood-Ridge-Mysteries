# R01 — Book 3 Ch 2: After the Lecture (lines 149–258)
**Running impression:** 4/5 · **DNF risk:** none

## Gut reaction (in Dottie's voice)
Oh, that lecture scene broke my heart — not from the grue, praise be, but from the way the whole town held its breath while Larter showed them Callie's fallibility. And then the discovery! My, my, a dead woman in a reading room with a marble bookend and that awful copper smell. Bless this author for restraint — no graphic details, just the careful, terrible aftermath. Callie reading the room like a page? Splendid. Cross crouching by the body, hat off, asking Callie to walk him through it? My knees went weak.

## What worked
* **Restrained murder:** "Copper smell... the only honest thing in the room" (2:219) — fair-play clue without grue.
* **Crime-scene reading:** Callie notices the folded cloth, the missing bookend, the overhead light wrong (2:225–227) — classic cozy detection.
* **Blank sign-out line** (2:223): a perfect, discoverable clue.
* **Cross's thaw:** "Walk this room with me... Not as a suspect. As the only person who can tell me what any of those documents mean" (2:247) — the latch lifting.
* **Larter's "I'd rather be wrong"** (2:197) — respect that makes her loss deeper.

## What didn't
* Larter is killed before we truly know her as a character — she's gone at 2:217, just pages after we met her fully alive at 2:197.
* The town's relief at Callie's "fallibility" (2:181–185) is uncomfortably realistic but makes Dottie's heart hurt.

## Persona checklist progress
* Callie likeable: **YES** — she respects the dead woman, reads honestly, doesn't dramatize.
* Cozy contract: **YES** — death off-page, no grue, body described with restraint (2:217–221).
* No gore/swearing/cruelty to elders: **YES** — clean through Ch 2.
