**Running impression:** 4/5 · **DNF risk:** none

## Gut reaction (in Dottie's voice)
Well now, that was honest and hard to watch. Callie finally admitting Larter was right — "It's real. Larter was right" — and seeing that second hand with her own glass. Bless her for not making it small, for saying her original reading was "incomplete" and that she was "edited, not simply wrong." The collaboration between her glass and Larter's tables is the kind of partnership that makes a teacher's heart sing. Even in grief, there's growth.

* **Glass + data collaboration:** "Together they make a shape neither method could make alone" (4:453) — fair-play puzzle mechanics.
* **Larter's margin note:** "Compare: County known-hand holdings, 1850s — fols. 43–44?" (4:465) — the breadcrumb that solves the mystery.
* **Callie's teaching:** She lets Eli notice the note himself (4:467–477) — gentle mentorship.
* **The catalogue skip confirmed:** "A catalog skip is not proof... it tells us something should be asked" (4:487–497).
