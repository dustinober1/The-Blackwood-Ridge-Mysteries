# R01 — Book 3 Ch 1: The Visitor Who Came Looking (lines 41–148)
**Running impression:** 5/5 · **DNF risk:** none

## Gut reaction (in Dottie's voice)
Well now, what a lovely way to reopen a mystery. Callie watching folks slow at her shop door the way they used to slow at hers — that almost-relief that's crueler than shunning (1:43–49). And the tea! Bergamot first, before the morning shapes itself into anything she has to answer (1:51). Then this marvelous visitor, Dr. Larter, arriving with her leather portfolio and her careful, careful way of handling Callie's work. My teacher's heart just melted.

## What worked
* **Pariah likeability:** Callie's not rude, not bitter — she's thoughtful, measured, generous even to the woman challenging her life's work (1:53, 1:85–87, 1:107, 1:129–131).
* **Larter's respectful entry:** "I'd rather show it to you than tell you" (1:61); removes glasses with "this part is me" gesture (1:111) — establishes her as challenger, not villain.
* **The second-hand premise:** Function-word ratios and clause-length shifts (1:73–81) — a fresh, fair mechanism for a cozy.
* **Glass as metaphor:** Eleanor's glass (1:83, 1:123) and "almost right, not quite" (1:145) — tactile and emotional.

## What didn't
* The town's "practiced" silence (1:49) means less warm community texture than Dottie prefers.
* Larter is a bit too perfect — careful, respectful, brilliant. She hasn't shown a human flaw yet.

## Persona checklist progress
* Callie likeable despite pariah status: **YES** — respectful to strangers, kind to elders.
* Cozy contract honored: **YES** — intellectual puzzle, no violence, tea rituals.
* No gore, swearing, animal cruelty, or rudeness to elders: **YES** through Ch 1.
