## Consensus issues (3+ personas)

1. **Repetitive motif tics** (A01, H01, M01, R05, I02 — 5 personas): tea/brass-glass motif 8× across 8 chapters (A01); bergamot refrain 6×, "forbidden question" 6×, Eleanor's ghost 3×, Eli's collar-touch 18×, rain 18× (H01); 20+ "not X but Y" constructions (A01); all 8 chapters end on a near-identical reflective beat (A01). Same cadence weakness as Book 2, now quantified.
2. **Mid-book procedural drag** (M01, L01, M02, R05, A01 — 5 personas): stylometry lecture is the densest block (R05: 1:69–105); Ch 2 lecture repeats Ch 1 evidence (I02: 171–191); Ch 6–7 sag between shelf discovery and finale (M01); access-policy read-through (R05: 6:871–887; I02: verbs read twice at 872, 962).
3. **Beech's confession too neat / too articulate** (L01, H01, R01, I02 — 4 personas): a 30-year record-smoother confessing in polished sentences (H01: 8:1519–1532); a book whose central lesson is "neat is the danger" arranging its own confession neatly (L01); ancestor phrase on-the-nose (I02: 8:1531).
4. **Fair-play clue ladder, culprit named early** (M01, A01, I02, H01 — 4 personas, strength): culprit named Ch 1 (115–117); bookend planted pre-murder (153); every deduction on the page (223, 349–351, 731–737); alternatives aired and answered (1115–1125, 1471–1476).
5. **The town's collective silence as antagonist** (R01, R05, I02, L01 — 4 personas, strength): "almost-relief" cruelty (1:47–49); the archive "too orderly around a hole."
6. **Cross's thaw and the "useful" arc** (R01, M01, I02 — 3 personas, strength): "I believe more of this than I want to" (7:1155); "your reading was useful" (8:1617–1621).

Same structure, opposite verdicts — the Book 2 split, repeated.

## Headline for the author

Book 3 earns its fair-play praise (clues on the page, culprit named early, model-not-magic) and its mid-book drag (Ch 2 lecture, Ch 6–7 sag) and motif repetition (tea/glass, bergamot, collar-touch) are the panel's consistent knocks. The Beech confession is the pivot: four personas find it too neat for a book whose lesson is "smoothness is the tell." If Book 4 fixes two things: cut the lecture, and let the confession resist its own smoothness.
