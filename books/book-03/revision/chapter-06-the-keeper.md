# Revision Notes — Chapter 6, The Keeper

2026-06-30 (revision round 1, v0.3.1, eighteen-pass)

## Integrated pass summary

Chapter 6 now functions as the access-and-opportunity narrowing chapter after the Chapter 5 midpoint proof. Callie returns to the County Historical Society with the receipts/shelf proof already established, performs ordinary research as cover, and reads Harlan Beech in the space he controls: access policy, key ledger, movement records, request slips, labels, coffee, and the desk he has made feel trustworthy.

The draft's strongest material was the still-hands beat and Mae's keeping/hiding mirror, but both were too explicit. The revised chapter lands Beech's hand-freeze once, under a specific procedural pressure point about after-hours access, and then lets the rest of the suspicion remain a reading rather than courtroom-ready proof. Mae's scene has been slowed and cooled so she sharpens the forbidden question without answering it for Callie.
