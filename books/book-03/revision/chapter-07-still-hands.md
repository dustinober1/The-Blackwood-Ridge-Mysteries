# Revision Notes — Chapter 7, Still Hands

2026-06-30 (revision round 1, v0.3.1, eighteen-pass)

## Integrated pass summary

Chapter 7 now functions as the all-is-lost procedural wall. Callie brings Cross the strongest case she can assemble — receipts, shelf photographs, movement/access records, alarm questions, the A. Beech estate certification, and the still-hands read — and Cross listens more fully than he would have in earlier books. The low point is not disbelief. It is worse: Cross believes more than Callie expected, and it still is not enough to support a warrant, formal accusation, or arrest.

The draft's core spine was strong, but it over-answered the forbidden question through Eli and overstated the Beech ancestor clue as if it already proved the amendment. The revision protects the burden of proof: the A. Beech certification gives family/access path only; Beech's hand tell is real to Callie but not probable cause; Eli steadies without solving; Chapter 8 retains confession, recovery, arrest, and the thematic answer.

## What changed

- Changed Chapter 7 status from draft to revised.
- Rebuilt the Sheriff's Office scene around the evidence Cross can hold.
- Clarified the A. Beech estate certification as family/access path only, not Document X or motive proof.
- Made Cross explicitly and painfully reasonable about probable cause, warrant limits, missing folios, and lack of murder proof.
- Removed Eli's over-complete answer to the forbidden question.
- Made Eli's contribution a grounded reframing: Cross needs to witness the reading rather than hear it secondhand.
- Ended with Callie's realization that Chapter 8 must assemble shelf, receipts, access, family clue, and hands in Beech's own reading room with Cross watching.

## What was intentionally preserved

- Callie single POV.
- Cross's procedural guardrail function without Book 1 dismissiveness.
- The Chapter 6 still-hands tell as already discovered, not newly staged.
- Eli's steady but limited support.
- The forbidden question as live pressure, not yet answered.
- No Document X recovery.
- No Document Y reveal.
- No warrant, arrest, detention, or formal accusation in Chapter 7.

## Conflicts reconciled

- **Belief vs. action:** Cross can believe Callie and still not have probable cause.
- **Family-name clue vs. proof:** A Beech at the clerk's desk is meaningful, but not enough to prove the amendment or motive.
- **Still-hands tell vs. legal evidence:** The tell is emotionally and narratively real but not probable cause.
- **Eli usefulness vs. steering risk:** Eli helps Callie distinguish unreadable-to-Cross from wrong; he does not solve the case or answer her moral question.
- **All-is-lost vs. forward motion:** The chapter lands the wall and then identifies the door into Chapter 8.
