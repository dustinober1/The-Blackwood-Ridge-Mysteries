# Carry-Forward into Book 3

> tight-mode summary of where the recurring characters physically and
> emotionally stand at the start of Book 3, what is resolved, and what is open.
> The per-book `/novella-concept` and `/novella-draft-chapter` skills must
> honor this. Source of truth: the published state of Book 2.

## Timeline position

Book 3 opens after the events of Book 2 ("The Botanical Confession"): Wells
Ambler arrested at Blackwater Cove, Lauren Coalter's remains recovered, the
town wounded a second time by Callie's hand. Eli's Saturday apprenticeship is
formalized. Mae knocked three times. The bergamot compass read "almost right.
Not quite."

## The town (setting state)

- Wounded a second time. A second pillar (after Gus Ralston) fallen to Callie's
  hand in a year: Wells Ambler, beloved principal.
- The garden club fractured, then voted to dissolve the Dabney floriography
  beds; cuttings dispersed into scattered yards.
- Callie remains "useful, not welcome" — the pariah state's hardest edge is the
  almost-relief that doesn't arrive (Harlan Beech slowed at her door, then
  swept past).
- The botanical book ("Flora's Lexicon of the Virginias," 1873) is now at the
  sheriff's office as evidence; provenance chain starts with Callie.

## Recurring characters — physical & emotional start state

### Callie Thorne (POV)
- **Physical:** Still owns The Foxed Page; lives in the apartment upstairs.
  Glass fully reclaimed ("Not Eleanor's glass. Hers"). Ink stains maintained;
  soil-under-nails fading from the Dabney garden.
- **Emotional:** Pariah state **confirmed as resolved** — neither rehabilitated
  nor crushed. Reclamation held under Book 2 pressure but did not deepen into
  belonging. Made an interior vow against Marguerite's silence: *not like this*
  — she will speak even when the speaking only burns.
- **Open wound/seed:** The forbidden question — "*Are you so different, Callie
  Thorne, from the man who buried it?*" — left standing, unanswered, "a stone
  in a current." Series-level recurring Callie-vow/wound: the cost of her own
  vocation (exhuming truth regardless of cost) mirrored against Wells's
  compulsion to cover.
- **Open thread (Book 3 engine):** Her Book 1 findings are about to be tested
  by a visiting academic who respected her work enough to challenge it.

### Sheriff Dalton Cross
- **Physical:** At the Sheriff's Office. Arrested his friend Wells Ambler in
  Book 2 — "the mechanical procedure of a man doing his duty over the ruins of
  a friendship." The friendship with Wells is destroyed; the professional cost
  of Book 2 sits on him.
- **Emotional:** Read the whole decoded file before acting (first time — new
  since Book 1). Did not shake Callie's hand at the close (the Book-1 handshake
  not repeated; the absence is the thing). Did not say *good work* or *thank
  you* or *I should have listened sooner*. His hand went to the back of his
  neck and stayed there through the recovery.
- **State:** Still a procedural guardrail — the door between him and Callie
  exists but is not opened. Book 3 slot: he "begins to consult her" (not
  formally, but the door opens). The hat-withholding tell tracks warming.
- **Open thread:** How he handles a case where Callie's Book 1 findings are
  directly challenged — does he defend her work or let the challenge stand?

### Mae Hartwell
- **Physical:** In her home (Hartwell House); the family Bible with its damning
  margin note still on the parlor shelf.
- **Emotional:** Chose to speak in Book 2 — eight months late but "earlier than
  Marguerite." Confessed Marguerite's October visit and the bet-on-Callie.
  Walked into Cross's office and said one word that moved him to act. Now
  knocks three times (asking permission) where she used to walk in.
- **State:** The fracture with Callie **might soften** — has not healed. "Earlier
  than Marguerite" is her Book-2 gift and absolution-without-absolution.
  Lavender "thinner now, strained" — the sachet-and-cinnamon smell registered
  as fading for the first time. Book 3 slot: "slow thaw." Track whether the
  calcification continues to soften or sets again.
- **Open thread:** Whether the surrogate-family bond heals, hardens, or quietly
  calcifies. Mae's promise to Eleanor still binds her even through the wound.

### Eli Townsend
- **Physical:** Saturdays at The Foxed Page — apprenticeship formalized ("You
  come in on Saturdays. I'll show you what I can"). Still at the Ridgeway
  Auction House during the week.
- **Emotional:** Standing straighter; his hand is now steady (new — "the
  steadiness was new," the anxiety-to-confidence evolution's next step).
  Collar-touching persists but reads as a badge, not an apology. "Past the
  *thank you*, into the place where a person just nods because the words have
  been said and the rest is the work."
- **State:** Series arc toward "de facto apprentice" now seeded and committed.
  Book 3 slot: "growing more confident, assisting Callie with research."
- **Open thread:** Father's debt — "Call when you've figured things out" —
  still unpaid. Accrues in the background.

## Resolved vs. open

**Resolved by Book 2:**
- Lauren Coalter's disappearance solved; Wells Ambler arrested; remains
  recovered from Blackwater Cove.
- Marguerite Dabney's floriographic cipher decoded and delivered; Marguerite
  vindicated ("not wrong, not a coward, just too late").
- The botanical book's unknown hand identified and its accusation made into
  evidence.
- Eli's Saturday apprenticeship formalized.
- Mae's micro-redemptive arc delivered ("earlier than Marguerite").

**Open into Book 3:**
- The validity of Callie's Book 1 findings (the academic's challenge — the Book
  3 engine).
- The Callie–Mae fracture (slow thaw? or does it set again?).
- Callie's standing in town (pariah; useful, not welcome — does the academic's
  external validation shift it?).
- Eli's trajectory toward "de facto apprentice" (Book 5 payoff per series
  outline).
- Whether bergamot tea "tastes right yet" under the new pressure of being
  *challenged* rather than merely resented.
- The forbidden question (Callie's cost-of-vocation wound) — does being
  challenged on her methods sharpen or soften it?

## Constraints for Book 3

- Do **not** re-expose the founding fraud (Silas Ralston's theft; Reverend
  Hartwell's murder) — that case is closed and public. The academic's challenge
  must concern Callie's *interpretation* of the Wren margin notes, not re-litigate
  the fraud itself.
- Do **not** fully rehabilitate Callie's town reputation in this book — the slot
  requires the shift from "pariah" to "grudging utility" (someone sought her
  out; Cross begins to consult her), not to acceptance.
- Do **not** formally partner Callie and Cross — that is the Book 4 slot. Book
  3 opens the door; it does not walk through it. Cross "begins to consult her"
  — not formally, but the door is open.
- Do **not** surface the puppet-master thread (Eli steering Callie) — that
  begins Book 5+. Book 3 plants only the *first* puppet-master seed: the gap in
  Callie's Book 1 work (a document she missed) that was removed by someone who
  didn't want her to find it. Callie does not yet know it was removed
  deliberately.
- Eli's role grows (assisting with research) but he is not yet "de facto
  apprentice" (Book 5). Track his confidence arc without accelerating it.
- The academic is a new character (not a series recurring) — they arrive,
  challenge, and are murdered. Treat them as the engine of Book 3, not a
  permanent addition to the cast.
