- Ebook manuscript: `The-Challenger.epub`
- Ebook cover: `The-Challenger-cover.jpg`

Both are generated together by `books/book-03/export/build.sh` and mechanically validated before the package ZIP is created.

## Commercial defaults from the established series strategy

- Launch list price: **$2.99**.
- Link the title to **The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries** as **Book 3**.
- Enroll in KDP Select / Kindle Unlimited only if continuing the Books 1 and 2 exclusivity strategy.
- Choose the release date or preorder date in the KDP dashboard.

## Required final platform checks

1. Upload the EPUB and separate cover image.
2. Open the KDP online previewer or Kindle Previewer.
3. Inspect the title page, copyright page, contents/navigation, all eight chapter starts, scene breaks, italics, ending, and back matter.
4. Confirm the cover is legible at thumbnail size and is not cropped.
5. Confirm the book description, keywords, categories, price, territories, DRM choice, and KDP Select choice.
6. Answer KDP's content-disclosure questions from the actual production history.
7. Submit for publication.

Do not mark `publish: complete` in the repository until Amazon has accepted the book and the detail page is live.
