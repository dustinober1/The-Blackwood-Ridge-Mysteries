# Publishing listing — The Annotated Murder

## Title clearance

### Pressure-test

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Length and rhythm | 3 words, literary rhythm. Shakespeare-derived register ("annotated" as modifier). Fits the elevated, atmospheric tone. |
| Genre signal | "Murder" signals mystery immediately; "Annotated" signals bookish/literary. Not instantly recognizable as cozy — more literary mystery — but the subtitle and cover close the gap. |
| Memorability | "Annotated" is an unusual word for a mystery title, which makes it distinctive. The flip from the saturated "Murder Most [X]" to "[The] Annotated Murder" creates a novel phrase. |
| Spoiler/theme alignment | Telegraphs the marginalia hook without revealing the turn. Sets the literary register accurately. |

### Collision check

| Candidate | Verdict | Evidence |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| The Annotated Murder | **CLEAR** | No exact match on Amazon, Goodreads, or WorldCat. No trademark. |
| Murder Most Annotated (working title) | CLEAR but RISKY | No exact match, but "Murder Most [X]" pattern has 335+ books on Amazon (285 in cozy alone). Near-match: *Murder Most Annoying* (John Duckworth, 2020) — low confusion risk. |

Alternatives considered:
- *The Foxed Page* — CLEAR, zero matches. Very distinctive, but loses the Shakespeare register and the word "annotated."
- *Murder in the Margins* — BLOCKED. 12+ exact matches in cozy mystery (Margaret Loudon trad-pub, 310 reviews).
- *Death in the Margins* — BLOCKED. Victoria Gilbert trad-pub cozy (300 reviews).
- *Murder Most Read* — CLEAR. Unique phrase but still in saturated "Murder Most" pattern.

### Decision

**The Annotated Murder** — legally clear, commercially distinctive, preserves the core "annotated" hook, and flips the saturated "Murder Most" pattern into a novel construction. The former working title "Murder Most Annotated" is retired.

## Book description

### Blurb (primary)

Some books hide their darkest secrets in the margins.

When a reclusive town historian is found poisoned at a rare book auction in the misty Blue Ridge foothills, antiquarian bookshop owner Callie Thorne is the only one who notices what the police missed: a coded message handwritten in the victim's catalog.

For Callie, marginalia isn't just an academic curiosity — it's a gift. Decoding the faded, handwritten clues tucked inside old books is what she does better than anyone. But this time the trail leads far deeper than a single murder. Every note points toward a decades-old scandal buried in Blackwood Ridge's founding families, and unraveling it means destroying the lives of the very neighbors she's trying to protect.

The sheriff sees a meddling shopkeeper. The killer sees a threat that needs silencing. And the town elders — people Callie has known her whole life — see a woman who refuses to stop digging.

With an innocent man already in the crosshairs, Callie faces an impossible choice: let a killer walk free, or expose the truth and watch her community shatter.

*Book 1 of The Blackwood Ridge Mysteries — a new series about the secrets hidden between the lines, and the women brave enough to read them.*

Perfect for readers who love atmospheric bookshop mysteries, literary amateur sleuths, and small-town secrets with real emotional stakes.

## Pricing guidance

**Recommended launch price: $2.99**

- $2.99 is the floor of the 70% royalty band (you earn ~$2.09 per sale vs. ~$0.35 at $0.99).
- The "$0.99 Book 1 loss leader" model works best for authors with 5+ published books. For a debut with no backlist, $0.99 signals low value and yields minimal revenue.
- All direct comps (*The Bookshop Murder*, *An Irish Bookshop Murder*, *Murder in Cherry Hills*) launch at $2.99.
- At 21,700 words (~87 KENPC pages), one full KU read = ~$0.39.
- Cozy readers are heavy KU consumers — enrollment is essential.
- If after 90 days you have fewer than 50 reviews and slow sales, consider a temporary $0.99 Kindle Countdown Deal promotion, then return to $2.99.
- Never price above $3.99 for a novella-length Book 1 with no established audience.
- Book 2+ in the series: $2.99–$3.99.
