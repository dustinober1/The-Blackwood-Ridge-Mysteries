# Book Packaging & Marketing Kit

Translates a finished manuscript into market-facing publishing assets: high-converting blurbs, traditional publishing query letters, retail keywords, and art briefs for cover illustrators/designers.

## Generated Assets

1. **Back-Cover Blurb Copy:**
   - Hook line, character dilemma, escalating stakes, cliffhanger payoff question.
   - 3 length variants: Micro (50 words for ads), Standard (150–200 words for back cover/retail), Expanded (with editorial comp titles).
2. **Traditional Query Letter:**
   - Personalization placeholder, logline, 250-word pitch paragraph, comparable titles (comps published in last 3 years), bio & target audience word count.
3. **Retail Metadata & Keywords:**
   - 7 Amazon search keyword phrases (long-tail search intent).
   - BISAC / Amazon category recommendations.
   - Taglines and series subtitle options.
4. **Cover Design Brief:**
   - Visual genre expectations and color psychology.
   - Focal point imagery, background setting cues, typography style guidance.
   - Avoidance list (elements that look generic, amateurish, or mismatch the tone).
