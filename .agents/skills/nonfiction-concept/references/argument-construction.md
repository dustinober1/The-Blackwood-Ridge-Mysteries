# Argument Construction for Nonfiction

## What Makes a Thesis Load-Bearing

A load-bearing thesis has three properties:

1. **Falsifiable** — It makes a claim that could be wrong. "Leadership is important" is not a thesis. "The most effective leaders in high-uncertainty environments delegate decision-making authority to the edges of the organization" is.

2. **Scoped** — It defines its boundaries. "How to be productive" is a topic. "How knowledge workers in distributed teams can achieve deep work without burnout by designing their environment for attention residue reduction" is a scoped thesis.

3. **Evidencable** — Every clause in the thesis maps to a type of evidence you can actually gather. If you claim "X causes Y," you need causal evidence. If you claim "X correlates with Y in context Z," you need comparative data.

## The Thesis Template

**Thesis = [Specific Claim] + [Boundary Conditions] + [Mechanism/Reason]**

Examples:
- Weak: "Habits determine success."
- Strong: "For knowledge workers, identity-based habits (who you believe you are) outperform outcome-based habits (what you want to achieve) because they reduce decision fatigue and survive motivation dips."

## Testing Your Thesis

Run these tests before committing:

| Test | Question | Pass Criteria |
|------|----------|---------------|
| **Falsifiability** | Could a reasonable person disagree with evidence? | Yes |
| **Scope** | Does it exclude something relevant? | Yes — it draws a line |
| **Evidence Map** | Can you name the evidence for each clause? | Yes — 1:1 mapping |
| **Reader Value** | Does the reader *need* this argument? | Solves a real problem |
| **Differentiation** | Is this the *only* book making this case? | Unique angle exists |

## Common Thesis Failures

- **Topic masquerading as thesis**: "This book is about negotiation." → Not an argument.
- **Truism**: "Communication matters in relationships." → Unfalsifiable.
- **Kitchen sink**: "Here are 50 tips for productivity." → No central argument.
- **Unbounded**: "How to think better." → No mechanism, no boundary.
- **Unprovable**: "The universe wants you to succeed." → No evidence architecture.

## From Thesis to Chapter Architecture

Once the thesis is locked, every chapter must:
- Advance one clause of the thesis
- Provide the evidence type mapped to that clause
- Address a specific reader objection to that clause
- End with a micro-conclusion that feeds the next chapter

If a chapter doesn't do this, it's not part of the argument — it's a tangent.
