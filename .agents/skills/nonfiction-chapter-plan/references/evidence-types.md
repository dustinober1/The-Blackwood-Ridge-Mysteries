# Evidence Types for Nonfiction Chapter Plans

## Primary Evidence Types

### Empirical
- Peer-reviewed studies, experiments, meta-analyses
- Quantitative data: statistics, measurements, survey results
- Qualitative data: coded interviews, observational field notes
- **Use when:** Proving causation, prevalence, effect size, correlation
- **Strength:** Verifiable, replicable, carries authority
- **Watch for:** Sample bias, p-hacking, overgeneralization from narrow samples

### Anecdotal / Case-Based
- Specific cases, narratives, exemplars, vignettes
- Personal experience (author's or subject's)
- Organizational case studies
- **Use when:** Illustrating mechanism, building intuition, humanizing abstract claims
- **Strength:** Concrete, memorable, emotionally resonant
- **Watch for:** Cherry-picking, representativeness, anecdote-as-data fallacy

### Theoretical / Conceptual
- Frameworks, models, principles, definitions
- Logical derivations, thought experiments
- Synthesis of existing theory
- **Use when:** Establishing premises, defining terms, showing structural necessity
- **Strength:** Clarifies thinking, reveals hidden assumptions
- **Watch for:** Untested abstraction, circular reasoning, jargon inflation

### Historical / Archival
- Primary sources: documents, letters, records, artifacts
- Secondary histories, historiographical debates
- Longitudinal patterns, institutional evolution
- **Use when:** Showing origins, contingency, path dependence, precedent
- **Strength:** Grounds claims in reality, reveals what's mutable
- **Watch for:** Presentism, selection bias, narrative imposition

### Counter-Argument / Steel-Manning
- Strongest objection to the claim
- Alternative explanation for the evidence
- Boundary conditions where claim fails
- **Use when:** Anticipating reader resistance, demonstrating intellectual honesty
- **Strength:** Increases credibility, sharpens the claim
- **Watch for:** Straw-manning, token concession, burying the objection

### Synthesis / Meta-Level
- Integration of multiple evidence types
- Pattern recognition across cases/studies
- New framework emerging from evidence
- **Use when:** Closing a chapter, bridging to next argument
- **Strength:** Creates new understanding, not just summary
- **Watch for:** Premature generalization, forced unification

## Evidence Combinations (Recommended)

| Primary | Supporting | Effect |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Empirical | Anecdotal | Data grounded in human reality |
| Theoretical | Historical | Abstract principle tested against record |
| Anecdotal | Counter-Argument | Story that survives its own critique |
| Historical | Empirical | Pattern confirmed by measurement |
| Synthesis | (any) | Argument advances to new level |

## Anti-Patterns

- **Survey laundry list:** 5+ studies cited for same point without synthesis
- **Anecdote stack:** Multiple stories substituting for argument
- **Definition drift:** Key term means different things in different blocks
- **Authority appeal:** "Experts say" without naming study/finding
- **Correlation parade:** Associations presented as causation
