# Ready-to-Adapt Structure Templates

Use these as starting grids. Replace thesis clauses, evidence types, and audience specifics.

---

## Template A: Problem-Solution (Standard)
**Best For:** Prescriptive thesis, Practitioner/Decision-Maker audience
**Chapter Count:** 14-18

### Part I: The Problem (Diagnosis) — Chapters 1-4
| Ch | Title Template | Type | Thesis Clause | Evidence | Milestone | Load |
|----|----------------|------|---------------|----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | The [Symptom] You're Living With | frame | Scope/Problem | Survey data, reader pain | Sees problem named | Low |
| 2 | Why [Common Fix] Fails | evidence | Root Cause | Case studies, failure analysis | Understands failure mode | Medium |
| 3 | The Hidden Cost of [Status Quo] | evidence | Cost of Inaction | Data, modeling, anecdotes | Feels urgency | Medium |
| 4 | The Paradigm Shift | synthesis | New Lens | Conceptual framework | Has new mental model | High |

### Part II: The Solution (The Thesis) — Chapters 5-9
| Ch | Title Template | Type | Thesis Clause | Evidence | Milestone | Load |
|----|----------------|------|---------------|----------|-----------|------|
| 5 | The Core Insight: [Big Idea] | frame | Mechanism 1 | Origin story, aha moment | Grasps big idea | Low |
| 6 | [Mechanism 1]: How [X] Works | evidence | Mechanism 1 | Primary evidence (study, data) | Believes mechanism 1 | High |
| 7 | [Mechanism 2]: How [Y] Works | evidence | Mechanism 2 | Primary evidence (study, data) | Believes mechanism 2 | High |
| 8 | Why This Beats [Alternative] | evidence | Comparative | Head-to-head, boundary map | Can defend choice | Medium |
| 9 | The Integrated Framework | synthesis | Mechanisms 1+2 | Visual integration | Sees full model | High |

### Part III: Implementation (Application) — Chapters 10-15
| Ch | Title Template | Type | Thesis Clause | Evidence | Milestone | Load |
|----|----------------|------|---------------|----------|-----------|------|
| 10 | Getting Started: The [Quick Win] | application | Outcome | Template, worked example | Ships first win | Medium |

## Template D: Hybrid — Problem-Solution + Chronological
**Best For:** Solution *is* a process (e.g., "How to build X in 90 days")
**Chapter Count:** 16-20

| Part | Ch Range | Focus | Type Mix |
|------|----------|-------|----------|
| I: Problem | 1-3 | Diagnosis | Frame, Evidence, Synthesis |
| II: The 90-Day Process | 4-15 | Chronological phases | Frame → Evidence → Application → Synthesis (per phase) |
| III: Mastery | 16-18 | Beyond | Application, Synthesis, Transition |

**Phase Template (repeats 3-4x):**
| Offset | Title | Type | Function |
|--------|-------|------|----------|
| +0 | Phase N: [Milestone] | frame | Orient |
| +1 | The [Mechanism/Skill] | evidence | Teach |
| +2 | The [Project/Sprint] | application | Do |
| +3 | [Retro/Integration] | synthesis | Integrate |

## Pacing Guides by Total Chapter Count

| Total Chapters | Problem-Solution | Chronological | Conceptual |
|----------------|------------------|---------------|------------|
| **12** | P1:3, P2:4, P3:5 | P1:3, P2:7, P3:2 | P1:4, P2:5, P3:3 |
| **14** | P1:4, P2:4, P3:6 | P1:4, P2:8, P3:2 | P1:5, P2:5, P3:4 |
| **16** | P1:4, P2:5, P3:7 | P1:4, P2:10, P3:2 | P1:5, P2:6, P3:5 |
| **18** | P1:5, P2:5, P3:8 | P1:4, P2:12, P3:2 | P1:5, P2:7, P3:6 |
| **20** | P1:5, P2:6, P3:9 | P1:5, P2:13, P3:2 | P1:6, P2:8, P3:6 |

**Rules:**
- Part I never > 30% of chapters
- Part III never < 15% of chapters
- Synthesis chapters: 1 per part minimum
- Transition chapters: invisible in count (500-1500 words each)
