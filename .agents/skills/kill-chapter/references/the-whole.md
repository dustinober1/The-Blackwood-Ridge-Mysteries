# Pass: kill "the whole of X" hollow intensifiers

Targets "the whole" when stretched over a pronoun or a rising sensation to inflate a
beat without adding a concrete image ("the whole of me", "the whole of his attention").

**The deletion-first rule:** prefer outright deletion over swapping in another intensifier.
Only do a fuller rewrite if deletion genuinely loses needed meaning, and ground it in a
concrete physical detail.

Examples:
- "the whole of me started to gather" → "I started to gather"
- "seized the whole of my attention" → "seized my attention"
- "the whole of the room tilted" → "the room tilted"

**KEEP:**
- Literal quantitative uses naming an actual complete set/place/duration: "the whole crew," "the whole room," "the whole night," "the whole town," "the whole plan."
- In-voice dialogue idioms: "that's the whole of it," "the whole business."

**FIX:**
- "the whole of me" / "the whole of him" / "the whole of my attention" / "the whole of it" in narration.
- 3+ uses of "the whole" within a few lines or a single scene.

Triage grep: `grep -rniE "\bthe whole of (me|him|her|it|my|his|them|our|attention)\b" <files>`
