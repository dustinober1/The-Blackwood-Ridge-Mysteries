## Common Nonfiction Fact Failures

Each pattern: symptom, why it fails, fix.

### Quote Drift
**Symptom:** Quote is 90% accurate — one word changed, a qualifier dropped, tense shifted.
**Why it fails:** Readers quote-check the memorable lines. One drift invalidates the rest.
**Fix:** Restore verbatim wording or convert to an attributed paraphrase.

### Statistic Without Denominator
**Symptom:** "50% improved" — 50% of what, versus what?
**Fix:** "50% of the 200 treatment participants improved (vs 30% control)."

### Correlation as Causation
**Symptom:** "X leads to Y" sourced to a study that only measured association.
**Fix:** Recast with correlational language, or add the experimental evidence for causation.

### The Phantom Citation
**Symptom:** "(Smith, 2021)" appears in the chapter but no Smith 2021 exists in the corpus.
**Fix:** Locate the real source or remove the citation; never leave a cite nobody can follow.

### Circular Sourcing
**Symptom:** Two chapters cite each other's claims; the original source is nowhere in the corpus.
**Fix:** Trace to the primary source or mark the claim proposed.

### False Precision
**Symptom:** "31.4%" when the source says "about 30%"; "over 10,000 years" for a rough estimate.
**Fix:** Match the source's precision.

### Stale Superlative
**Symptom:** "the largest ever" verified against a subset, or dated.
**Fix:** Verify against the whole corpus and date the claim: "the largest recorded as of [year]."

### Context Stripping
**Symptom:** Real quote, but the source's hedging or contrary condition is cut.
**Fix:** Restore the qualifier or drop the quote.

### Name and Date Slip
**Symptom:** Misspelled researcher, wrong year, study attributed to the wrong lab.
**Fix:** Corpus spelling wins. Re-check the year against the source's own publication record.

### Population Swap
**Symptom:** Study of undergraduates cited for a claim about all adults; mouse study cited for humans.
**Fix:** State the population explicitly, or find a source matching the claim's population.

### Anecdote Inflation
**Symptom:** One vivid case presented as the typical or representative outcome.
**Fix:** Add base rates, or scope the claim to the case itself.

### Definition Drift
**Symptom:** A locked defined term (from the chapter plan's continuity table) is used with a different meaning.
**Fix:** Restore the locked definition or flag the chapter plan for revision.

### The Borrowed Number
**Symptom:** A number cited in an earlier chapter reappears here slightly altered.
**Fix:** Cross-chapter consistency check: one canonical form per fact.

### Author-as-Authority
**Symptom:** "Research shows" / "studies indicate" with no named study.
**Fix:** Name the study, author, year, and key finding, or cut the claim.
