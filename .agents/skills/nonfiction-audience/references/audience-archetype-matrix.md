# Audience Archetype Matrix

## Archetype × Concept Compatibility

| Archetype | Best-Fit Thesis Type | Worst-Fit Thesis Type | Key Compatibility Signal |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Practitioner** | "How to do X better/faster/cheaper" with immediate application | Pure theory, abstract frameworks without worked examples | "I need this for my [meeting/project/decision] tomorrow" |
| **Aspirant** | "How to become X" with clear pathway, milestones, portfolio pieces | Advanced specialization, assumes insider knowledge | "I want to break into / level up to [role/field]" |
| **Decision-Maker** | "Why X matters for [outcome Y]" with decision criteria, risk framework | Tactical detail, implementation minutiae | "I need to approve / fund / kill this initiative" |
| **Curious Generalist** | "The surprising truth about X" with narrative, synthesis, big picture | Jargon-heavy, assumes domain fluency | "I want to understand [phenomenon] at dinner-party depth" |
| **Skeptic/Critic** | "The evidence for/against X" with rigorous methodology, limitations | Advocacy, cherry-picked anecdotes, unsupported claims | "Convince me — show your work and your failures" |

## Knowledge Level × Archetype Norms

| Archetype | Typical Knowledge Level | Prerequisite Tolerance | Depth Expectation |
|-----------|------------------------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| Practitioner | Practitioner → Expert | Low — show me the shortcut | Deep-dive on application; reference on theory |
| Aspirant | Novice → Some exposure | High — teach me the prerequisites | Scaffolded deep-dive; progressive complexity |
| Decision-Maker | Some exposure → Practitioner | Medium — brief me on essentials | Executive summary + decision framework; reference appendices |
| Curious Generalist | Novice | Medium — define terms in context | Narrative synthesis; conceptual depth without jargon |
| Skeptic/Critic | Expert | Low — I'll check your citations | Reference-grade rigor; methodology transparency |

## Objection Patterns by Archetype

| Archetype | Top Objection Themes | Response Strategy |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Practitioner | "Too theoretical" / "Won't work in my context" / "No time for this" | Worked examples from similar contexts; time-boxed quick wins; context adaptation guide |
| Aspirant | "Too advanced" / "Missing steps" / "How do I know I'm doing it right?" | Scaffolded exercises; checklists; milestone markers; feedback loops |
| Decision-Maker | "Not strategic enough" / "ROI unclear" / "Risks not addressed" | Decision framework; risk matrix; scenario planning; executive summary upfront |
| Curious Generalist | "Too dense" / "Lost the thread" / "So what?" | Signposting; narrative anchors; "why this matters" refrains; visual summaries |
| Skeptic/Critic | "Methodology flawed" / "Sample biased" / "Alternatives ignored" | Pre-registration; limitations section; steel-manned counter-arguments; open data |

## Credibility Signals by Archetype

| Archetype | Must-Have Signals | Nice-to-Have Signals |
|-----------|------------------|---------------------|
| Practitioner | Recent, relevant experience; shipped artifacts; peer adoption | Named companies; measurable outcomes; templates included |
| Aspirant | Clear pathway; beginner-friendly voice; community access | Mentor stories; portfolio templates; job market data |
| Decision-Maker | Decision frameworks; risk analysis; peer case studies | Board-level experience; regulatory awareness; budget models |
| Curious Generalist | Narrative fluency; synthesis breadth; "aha" moments | Cross-domain connections; historical context; further reading map |
| Skeptic/Critic | Transparent methodology; registered reports; null results published | Adversarial review; replication data; pre-commitment analysis |

## Conflict Detection Rules

**Flag as CONFLICT when:**
- Practitioner audience + thesis requires 6 months study before first application
- Aspirant audience + prerequisites include "5+ years experience in X"
- Decision-Maker audience + book is 90% narrative with no decision framework
- Curious Generalist audience + every chapter assumes prior chapter's jargon
- Skeptic/Critic audience + evidence is primarily author anecdotes

**Flag as TENSION when:**
- Practitioner audience + some theoretical depth needed for transferability
- Aspirant audience + advanced optional sections for stretch goals
- Decision-Maker audience + technical appendix for due diligence
- Curious Generalist audience + one deep-dive chapter for the curious
- Skeptic/Critic audience + practical implications section

**ALIGNED when:** Archetype norms match thesis demands and structural plan.
